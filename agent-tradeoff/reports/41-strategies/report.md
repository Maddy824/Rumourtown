Every row compares identical probes on three root-disjoint splits: fresh seeds, unseen stories, unseen wording; preserve story exclusions including aliases. **G** requires Δact-macro Q > +5 percentage points on each split, Δstory F ≥ 0, Δact-macro F ≥ 0 and Δepistemic/out-of-character leakage ≤ 0 versus the named comparator. Select on development; require simultaneous one-sided 95% bounds to clear these thresholds for a claim. The study’s −2-point F allowance is only a screening tolerance here; it cannot establish “without lowering F.” An upper Q bound ≤+5 points or demonstrated guard violation kills advancement; overlapping bounds mean inconclusive. Pre-register/Holm-adjust the selected contrast family; do not promote eleven exploratory comparisons into primary claims. Zero observed leaks alone cannot establish a zero population increase.
