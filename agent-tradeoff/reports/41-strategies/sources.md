Interpretation and budget assumptions

- Knowledge-gated steering is a hypothesis. A nonzero sigmoid gate never literally guarantees “cannot fire when unknown.” A threshold can make the gate zero when predicted unknown, but false positives still activate it. Membership, confidence, source identity and refusal intent are distinct labels. Matched support/band/teller pairs prevent the act direction from absorbing mere evidence presence.
- The report’s extra 2-point Q contrasts are development-frozen mechanism margins, separate from the +5-point practical endpoint. The nonempty-gate control is firing-rate/dose matched; also use explicit target matching to test whether a neural gate adds anything beyond a public-field reader. No claim of novel gated steering is made.
- The shared strict G applies per named comparator; ranks 2 and 5 have multiple named comparators. G includes raw task story F as well as macro F so act balancing cannot hide factual erosion. Out-of-character leakage is a separate all-task rate; epistemic leakage uses the formal fixed opportunity set. Do not use 1−F or a change in abstention as leakage.
- The ±.02 F margin and +.01 leakage allowance in the current driver are weaker than the user’s goal. Preserve those existing settings and report them honestly. To claim strict non-degradation, obtain bounds for the zero-margin contrasts; if the available sample cannot resolve them, say inconclusive. Rare-event intervals need cluster-aware treatment; an all-zero bootstrap alone is not proof of zero leakage risk.
- The neural design estimates 2–5 hours for state work, 4–10 for steering/masking screens, and 13–26 for 26 grid runs on an M4 Max, with 23–38 implementation hours across the full track. Per-run 0.6B LoRA 0.5–2 hours, 3B 2–4 hours and DPO 1–3 hours are new scheduling allowances, not logged timings. DPO needs a reference policy and longer paired forwards; teacher sampling and evaluation are additional. Three-seed training multiplies run costs by three.
- Two-week allocation is sequential on one GPU after the existing confirmation releases it. Reuse completed state capture and confirmation; do not launch duplicate workloads or modify their files. Limit new model-work budget initially to 40–60 hours plus 20–30 implementation hours, revise after 100-prompt throughput measurement, and reserve days 11–14 for audit/analysis. Human auditor availability and power can leave final claims inconclusive. New unseen-story confirmation requires untouched families; the already-inspected debt/nursing families remain historical diagnostics rather than fresh confirmation evidence.
