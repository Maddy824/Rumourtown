# Research strategy review

Date: 22 October 2026. Recommendation: make the benchmark and its measurement validity the first contribution; make useful, transferable boundary compliance the research question. Treat filtered LoRA training as one intervention, whose value remains unproved.

This is a strategy document, not an experimental result. I read the requested study, both literature reviews, paper plan, limits program, product-impact notes, harness, types, probes, verifier and training statistics, and additionally inspected scoring, prompts, filtering, training, annotation and budget documentation. The six runs and their loss trends are the task's supplied observations; I do not assume outcomes from the pending paired evaluation. Reviewer reactions, rankings, sample targets and effort estimates below are my judgments, not venue requirements or measured performance.

## Measurement problems to settle first

1. **Exact state does not imply an exact text oracle.** The verifier recognizes claims through cast names, an event regex, lexical overlap, polarity and simple argument roles. Unnamed subjects, amounts, paraphrases, compound claims and implications can escape or confuse it. An entirely empty/chatter reply can be epistemically faithful and calibrated. The development set's 20/20 core and 2/4 hard cases are development observations, not held-out validity. [Verifier](../../apps/rumortown/src/agentic/verifier.ts), [notes](../../docs/research/verifier-notes.md)
2. **World faithfulness is currently conditional on epistemic faithfulness.** Code computes `!bad && supportedFacts.every(f => f.trueInWorld)`. A true statement unknown to the speaker fails both scores; the unknown-world match does not independently decide truth. Before advertising two independent axes, define a separate claim-level world-truth reference, including unknown truth, mixed claims and vacuous nonanswers. Publish all four truth/belief cells and unanswered rates; retain the old score under its original definition for comparability. [Verifier](../../apps/rumortown/src/agentic/verifier.ts)
3. **Provenance and calibration can win by omission.** The source check accepts a recognized name anywhere in the supplied chain, and missing/unrecognized sources can remain `null`; the gate accepts `null`. Provenance probes construct multi-hop chains, whereas ordinary snapshots now use direct-source context. Score required-source coverage and exact direct teller separately from valid ancestry, with explicit denominators. Calibration needs a no-claim/N/A category, not automatic credit. [Probes](../../bench/slm/probes.ts), [gate](../../bench/slm/strategies.ts)
4. **“Mechanism transfer” is currently narrower than its name.** Debt and nursing are two excluded authored story families, not many independently sampled causal mechanisms. Different seeds still reuse claims, names and wording. Seed gains prove neither abstract skill nor memorisation; story holdout adds evidence but cannot identify which lexical or relational feature transferred. Keep its current split name, qualify the interpretation, and add withheld relations, entities and wording families. [Harness](../../bench/slm/README.md)
5. **Twelve axes do not establish twelve capabilities.** Fact sweeps cap at nine available beliefs; repeated acknowledgement histories are not autonomous long conversations; gold acts are proxies, and a proposed action is not executed. The injection metric combines marker appearance with epistemic failure, so harmless quotation and unrelated error can affect it. Audit goal achievement, protected disclosure and benign quotation separately, with clean/attacked pairs. [Harness](../../bench/slm/README.md), [probes](../../bench/slm/probes.ts), [scoring](../../bench/slm/run.ts)
6. **Current filters change more than quality.** Training sizes are 1,435, 1,287 and 719, with different state/act coverage. Validation is separated by snapshot, but related ticks from the same simulation seed may cross that boundary. An epoch-three rebound suggests checkpoint selection, not proof that two epochs or a method is best. Use whole-seed validation and validation-only checkpoint selection; preserve the already exposed tests as exploratory. [Filtering](../../bench/slm/finetune/make-data.ts), [training](../../bench/slm/finetune/train.sh)

This is a scoped search finding, not an absence proof. Do not claim “first knowledge-boundary tuning,” “first verifier distillation,” “first asymmetric agent benchmark,” or “first paired abstention benchmark.” The defensible gap is the particular controlled, audited evaluation and any transfer result it produces. The close precedents are [RoleMRC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11387), [Muses-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.08567v2), [Ground-GRPO](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15522v1) and [AgentAbstain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10059).

## Three ranked directions

### 1. Release an audited benchmark of useful generation within explicit boundaries

**Claim it could support:** models that look faithful under a rejection filter may fail to provide supported, relevant answers; a controlled benchmark separates that failure from world-truth errors, source errors and silence. A null training result can still support a benchmark paper if the task is valid and baselines are informative. The contribution must be the inspectable state, false-belief dialogue and audited measurements; counterfactual context, source/confidence layers and paired abstention already exist. [FaithEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03727), [Truth for believable AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26035), [AgentAbstain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10059)

**Minimum experiment:** construct 100 matched pairs on each village test split, with state/question held fixed except for one intervention: speaker knows/does not know, source A/B, confidence high/low, or current/obsolete account. Include false-but-believed and true-but-unknown claims. Balance answerable and abstention sides. Evaluate base, current selected adapter, templates, cite/plan, and verifier-at-inference; use the same pairs across conditions. Score both sides correctly answered, claim validity and useful coverage, with a human audit. Treat this 200-pair exercise as a pilot until cluster counts and uncertainty justify stronger claims.

**Needs:** roughly 10–12 owner hours for task design, documentation and analysis, plus annotation time described below. The M4 Max can reuse existing 0.6B/1B/3B inference sequentially; no new training or cloud spend is necessary to establish the benchmark. Release versioned generators, frozen datasets/manifests, source definitions, all raw candidates, errors and analysis instructions; check model/data redistribution terms. Start with a static results table and submission format, not a hosted leaderboard service. Retain an unseen generated challenge set for future submissions.

**Risk and venue:** the evaluator may reward matching its vocabulary; joint truth/belief labels may be invalid; authored stories may be too homogeneous. Fix those before adding models. Best immediate fit: AIIDE/CoG technical paper, or an ACL evaluation/dialogue workshop. A public release by itself is not a contribution without validated measurements and useful findings.

### 2. Test cross-domain transfer with a small synthetic bank

**Claim it could support:** a village-trained adapter transfers evidence-scoped answer behaviour to a second synthetic domain, or fails in an interpretable way; training the same method on bank data tests method portability separately. Even successful transfer would not establish bank readiness. Existing bank detection and access-scoped generation make domain naming alone insufficient. [Banking privacy detector](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10222068/1/FinanSE_Kong_cameraReady.pdf), [access-controlled RAG](https://dbs-research.github.io/pdf/2025_sac.pdf)

**Minimum experiment:** generate 100 held-out bank pairs from independent customer/record seeds, covering customer A/B, posted/pending/reversed entries, amended documents, direct-source provenance, advisory/research division barriers, entitlement revocation, and document-borne injections. Keep the `FactRef` core (id, text, confidence, source, world truth); add recipient, access/purpose labels, status and version as explicit metadata. A pending payment is a known pending record, not a low-confidence posted payment. Define synthetic policies directly rather than claiming they encode banking law. Use no real customer data.

**Essential distinction:** epistemic fidelity checks what the agent knows; authorization checks what this recipient may receive. A known, true, forbidden fact can be perfectly epistemically faithful and still leak. Test (i) the shared-schema task where deterministic entitlement projection supplies only allowed evidence, and (ii) an explicitly labelled mixed-context stress test with known-but-forbidden records and an injected document. The second asks a new permission-following skill that village training did not teach. Use a separate access oracle; do not relabel all privacy failures “unknown-to-speaker.” This distinction is motivated by [Muses-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.08567v2) and [Purpose IR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16414).

**Comparisons:** base, frozen village adapter, bank-trained adapter and mixed-domain adapter, all with identical bank prompts and matched training budgets where applicable. Evaluate village-trained → bank without bank examples or bank-based checkpoint choice first. Compare with a bank-trained positive control and, if time permits, bank-trained → village. Test renamed entities, new predicates and a separately worded bank subset; a noun substitution alone demonstrates schema reuse, not domain generalization. For the full system compare entitlement filtering plus base/templates against entitlement filtering plus adapter. Score semantic disclosure, authorized task completion and provenance; include paraphrased/derived disclosures and multi-turn carryover, not only secret-string matches. Runtime isolation has close prior in [Harness-MU](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.21856v1).

**Needs:** 8–12 owner hours for a pilot and 1–2 hours of a banking practitioner's review if available; without that person call it an artificial record-access task. Local 3B LoRA and 4–8B quantized teacher inference should be tested sequentially on 36 GB; start with a small memory/throughput pilot, short sequences and batch one. Training/inference wall time must be measured, not promised. No local 70B training. New bank training and mixed-domain runs are stretch work after the zero-shot transfer pilot.

**Risk and venue:** a reused generator/verifier can create fake transfer, and a general realization skill may not transfer policy reasoning. Explicitly report zero or negative transfer and keep training/inference enforcement effects separate. Strongest longer-term industry value; CODS-COMAD applied/data work or an ACL finance/privacy workshop after semantic validation. A bank pilot would still require evaluation on its own policies and records; this study is not legal compliance evidence.

### 3. Repair target coverage, then test whether tuning beats inference controls

**Claim it could support:** controlling act coverage and target quality improves useful compliant answers rather than merely teaching abstention. If that fails, the narrower conclusion is that the current teacher/filter does not deliver the needed targets; model size alone has not been isolated. Answer/citation/refusal optimization is established, so novelty would be the controlled result. [Ground-GRPO](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15522v1)

**Minimum experiment:** begin with Qwen3-0.6B for the three-filter ablation; replicate only the selected comparison on deployable Llama-3.2-3B. Preserve the original distributions as descriptive arms. Add count/update-matched unfiltered, verifier-only and verifier-plus-act arms, and an act-balanced, semantically audited target arm. Set the shared quota using rare-act availability; aim for at least 50 distinct eligible train states per act and report any shortfall. Do not manufacture diversity by copying a handful of accepted source replies. Track per-act sample yield, unique states and total teacher tokens so the cost of rejection remains visible.

**Target repair order:** (1) author multiple deterministic source/hedge/denial/action templates grounded in exact state; (2) compare template-only and balanced natural-language targets on the same prompts, sizes and update counts, holding out realization templates; (3) sample an existing local 4–8B instruction teacher only for rare/failed acts, after checking its output mode and memory; (4) try one round of student rejection sampling if its rare-act yield is sufficient. Keep a common-prompt subset to distinguish filter effects from exclusion of harder states; report full-pool coverage too. A changed teacher, data balance and optimizer must not all change in one claimed comparison.

**DPO option:** only after SFT and the audit are sound, build same-prompt pairs where the chosen reply is supported, relevant and on-act, and the rejected reply is a specific unsupported answer or an unnecessary refusal. Include both error directions, match lengths where feasible, and manually validate pair labels. A scalar “oracle accepts” preference would reproduce silence. Compare SFT → DPO with SFT continued for the same update budget. Skip GRPO in the initial fortnight: online rollouts add engineering and amplify an unvalidated proxy. Exact-verifier self-training does not guarantee expanded capability. [Teacher-free self-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07856)

**Needs:** 6–10 owner hours excluding annotation; local sequential LoRA runs using the existing training stack, validation-selected checkpoints and three training seeds for the final comparison. Profile one run before budgeting overnight jobs; DPO needs policy/reference memory and is optional if existing tooling cannot support it without new dependencies. A remote stronger teacher is unnecessary. Rare targets need human semantic review, not just passing the training gate.

**Risk and venue:** templates may teach scoring phrases, weighting may overfit duplicates, and the helpfulness gain may disappear under independent paraphrases. Prefer a clearly bounded negative result to more optimizers. Fits an ACL efficient/dialogue/alignment workshop, with AIIDE/CoG as a practical adapter study if live-format gains survive. Workers AI lists Llama-3.2-3B LoRA support; its documented adapter requirements still need deployment-format validation. [Model](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/llama-3.2-3b-instruct/), [LoRA requirements](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/features/fine-tunes/loras/)

## Experimental rigour checklist

- **Freeze the question and analysis.** Primary endpoint: act-macro-averaged, independently audited useful-and-compliant success, including appropriate abstention on genuinely unanswerable requests. Report all component scores. Prespecify the selected tuning-versus-base contrast on each split, a useful-answer noninferiority margin (provisionally 3 percentage points) and an improvement target (provisionally 5 points); choose final margins using development data and practical needs, before fresh tests. Already inspected test data support exploratory conclusions only.
- **Independent units and repeats.** Hold out whole root simulation seeds, all their snapshots and paired variants; retain separate held-out stories/relations and a fresh confirmation set. Target at least 50 root seeds per split and three decoding seeds at the operating temperature. Use three LoRA seeds for the final method/base comparison in two families; do broad screening with one seed and label it screening. At temperature zero, identical repeated calls add no independent scenarios. Pin model revisions, tokenizer, chat format, precision, verifier, generation code, data hashes and decoding settings; record provider seed limitations.
- **Intervals and multiplicity.** Compute paired differences on shared probes, then 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals resampling root seeds with snapshots, sweep levels, counterfactual pairs and decoding draws kept together. Show scenario-clustered sensitivity results; account for training-run variation separately, with per-run effects and hierarchical resampling rather than treating all replies as independent. Only two withheld story families cannot support a population-level interval over mechanisms. Prespecify the small primary contrast family and use Holm correction; show other axes as exploratory with intervals. Estimate required clusters from development variance/paired discordance; 50 seeds is a target, not a power guarantee.
- **Concrete non-training baselines.** Always-abstain; deterministic state-to-text templates; persona-only; strong grounded prompt; fixed development few-shot prompt; SimToM-style perspective filtering; cite/schema-constrained output; plan-and-realize; teacher Qwen3-4B; and `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast` as a budgeted large-model reference. In bank tasks include deterministic entitlement projection plus templates/base. Full village grounding is already an oracle evidence-retrieval condition; do not call its score end-to-end RAG quality. The perspective baseline follows [SimToM](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.451/).
- **Verifier at inference.** Compare first-passing best-of-4 with useful-act-aware selection under the same four-candidate budget; include best-of-1 and an optional best-of-8 curve. The deployed selector must infer needed behaviour from input, not read test gold acts or world truth. Gold-aware selection is a labelled upper bound only. Compare a small→large verified cascade on a capped subset; report every rejected call, failure, fallback, exhausted selection and post-guard answer. Keep raw model, selector and guarded-system results separate. Match token/latency budgets as well as candidate counts.
- **Training controls.** Match examples, optimizer updates, prompt support, act mix and target length where the hypothesis requires them; report unavoidable differences and selection coverage. Compare unfiltered/verifier/act filters, balanced templates, balanced teacher targets, refusal-only SFT and one-round self-distillation as resources allow. Include a wrong-state training control only on development data to test content versus boundary dependence. Choose checkpoints and DPO settings only on whole-seed validation; report held-out general instruction-following and dialogue capability retention.
- **Human audit.** Target at least 500 distinct outputs, independently labelled by two people, blinded to model/method and oracle verdict, stratified across acts, splits, oracle labels, attacks and source/calibration cases. Reuse the existing annotation tool for belief fidelity, task completion and confidence; it does not independently annotate world truth or bank permissions. Add a separate reference-guided audit for those dimensions and compound-claim segmentation. Include omissions, answer relevance, exact source and whether a refusal was needed. Use a disjoint pilot to refine instructions, lock independent exports, report agreement, adjudication and per-label precision/recall with cluster intervals. Preserve disagreements instead of letting the oracle settle them. [Annotation limits](../../bench/slm/label/README.md)
- **Make the audit feasible and honest.** In two weeks one owner can initially label 200–250 outputs; a second independent rater and the remaining labels are a publication prerequisite, not assumed labour. Double-labelling oneself is not independence, and an LLM judge is only auxiliary. The existing sampler deliberately oversamples rare strata and reports unweighted results: use its population/sample counts for appropriate weighting before population accuracy claims; report rare-stratum diagnostics separately. Owner-designed bank policies need an independent domain review before industry-validity claims. [Audit implementation](../../bench/slm/label/README.md)
- **Make silence lose.** Report correct supported answers / answerable requests; unnecessary abstentions / answerable requests; appropriate abstentions / unanswerable requests; abstention precision/recall; requested-act macro success and each act's denominator; claim precision and required-content recall; source-required accuracy including omissions and source coverage; and human useful-answer ratings. Treat empty/chatter replies as zero completion on answerable tasks. Show risk–coverage curves with thresholds fixed on development data, and error at matched useful-answer coverage. An always-abstain system gets zero answerable coverage and zero both-sides pair success.
- **Separate other endpoints.** Report four world-truth/belief cells independently, contradiction/unknown/out-of-character errors, calibration conditional on factual replies plus its coverage, actual attack-goal success, and benign false refusals. For banks report known-forbidden disclosure, cross-customer and cross-division leakage, unauthorized confirmation of existence, stale-status errors and sanctioned task completion. Multi-turn leakage includes persistent memory/handoffs; string markers are diagnostics, not semantic privacy proof. Record end-to-end p50/p95, peak memory, all input/output tokens and local wall time; report hosted costs separately and count timeout/protocol failures as failed tasks in system success rates.
- **Contamination and transfer controls.** Never expose `trueInWorld`, hidden world facts, gold acts or private audit keys to generator prompts. Verify that training/validation share no root seed, snapshot, exact or near-duplicate dialogue, target realization family or held-out relation. Deduplicate and compare normalized/delexicalized text; regenerate fresh held-out names, identifiers, sources and facts after freezing the method. Hold out attack families and injection markers, not just seeds. Separate schema-preserving renaming, new vocabulary, new relations and actual bank policies. Log any test inspection and teacher exposure; do not regenerate failed test items into training. Novel private simulation instances reduce direct answer contamination but do not erase shared-template leakage.
