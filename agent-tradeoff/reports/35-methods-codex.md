# Methods: useful speech within an agent's knowledge boundary

This section specifies a confirmatory study; proposals below are not completed experiments. It follows the corrected October research plan and the Phase 0 report on `research/phase0`, rather than treating screening proxies as audited outcomes. No implementation changes are required by this document.

## State, policy and oracle

A root seed $z$ determines a simulation trajectory $W_z(t)$, including events, agents and a world valuation $T_z(f)\in\{0,1,?\}$ for propositions $f$. The valuation follows the authored account, including scheduled events where the benchmark so defines truth; it is independent of any agent's belief. Unknown reference truth is retained as unknown.

Agent $i$ has a ledger $L_i(t)=\{(k,f,c,b,S,v)\}$: stable identifier $k$, proposition $f$, confidence $c$, band $b\in\{\text{sure, fairly sure, half, doubt}\}$, direct sources $S$, and revision/version $v$. Direct sources identify who told this speaker, or a witnessed event, rather than every ancestor in a diffusion chain. A low-confidence belief licenses qualified language or doubt, not an assertion of certainty. Believed false propositions can be voiced faithfully.

An entitlement map $E_{i\to r}(t)\subseteq L_i(t)$ specifies disclosures to recipient $r$. In the village $E=L$ by design. In the bank $E$ may be a strict subset: a known fraud flag can be forbidden to a customer. Permission is evaluated over semantic disclosures, including existence, paraphrases and derived information, using explicit synthetic rules for status, version, revocation and conversational carry-over; checking mentioned record identifiers alone is insufficient.

The public policy input is $x=(P_i,R(L_i\text{ or }E_{i\to r}),D,H,u,r)$: persona, rendered evidence, explicitly marked distractors, dialogue history, current utterance and recipient information where applicable. Revisions accompany the rendering. Distractors and quoted instructions do not extend the speaker's knowledge. The policy $\pi_\theta(y\mid x)$ produces a reply or an error outcome $\bot$. World truth and gold labels are excluded from policy inputs. In the bank's mixed-context condition, forbidden known records remain visible but are marked with access information.

Gold $g(x,L,E)=(a,k^*,h^*,s^*)$ specifies the requested act and, where applicable, target fact, confidence expression and direct source. Acts are assert, hedge, deny, abstain, name the source, refuse, and propose an action. Gold is derived from the request and effective ledger, not merely world truth. Let $B=\{a\in\{\text{assert, hedge, deny, name the source}\}\}$ denote answerable requests, following Phase 0; refusal/action usefulness has separate act denominators.

The textual oracle is $V(x,y;L,E)=(\{(s_j,\ell_j,k_j)\}_{j=1}^{m},C,S_r,S_d,S_a,J)$, where labels $\ell_j$ are supported, contradicts, unknown-to-speaker, chatter or out-of-character. Checks return calibration $C$, required-source coverage $S_r$, exact direct teller $S_d$, valid ancestry $S_a$, and bank access violations $J$. Hereafter $V(x,y)$ abbreviates access to the evaluation sidecar; the policy does not receive that sidecar. The oracle also reports independently matched world truth. It is a classifier, not an exact semantic proof system.

Let $M$ mean a completed reply and $P$ valid nonempty protocol/citations. Define raw epistemic faithfulness $F(x,y)=\mathbf1\{\text{no contradicts, unknown-to-speaker or out-of-character sentence}\}$ on completed replies. Define $G$ as absence of calibration error and passage of the applicable Phase 0 source gates. No-claim calibration is N/A, but absence of a calibration error permits legitimate nonfactual acts. Missing requested sources fail source diagnostics; the documented bare-abstention exception permits honest ignorance without giving source-coverage credit. This inference gate does not inspect gold; only a gold-abstain request can earn completion from abstention. Valid ancestry is reported separately and never substitutes for a direct teller.

Let $A(x,y,g)$ be substantive gold-act completion, including the target supported fact when required; an empty or chatter-only factual reply has $A=0$. To make $Q=F\cdot U$ exactly match Phase 0, define admissible act completion $U=\mathbf1_M P G A$. Thus $A$ is the pure act measure and $U$ includes the other acceptance conditions. Equivalently, $Q=(\mathbf1_M P F G)A$: accepted and on-act. Set $F=U=Q=0$ on $\bot$ for task-level rates; retain the completed-reply legacy $F$ separately. Multiplying raw epistemic fidelity by a bare surface-act proxy without $P,G$ would give a different endpoint.

## Estimands and denominators

For split $s$, let $\mathcal D_s$ be the prespecified distribution of non-skipped requested probes and $Y\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid X)$. Its empirical analogue weights requested probes equally, including errors. Under random root seeds this is the ratio estimand $\mathbb E_Z[\sum_{p\in Z}h_p]/\mathbb E_Z[N_Z]$, not an unannounced average of seed percentages. Let $\langle h\rangle_C=\mathbb E_{\mathcal D_s,\pi}[h\mathbf1_C]/\Pr_{\mathcal D_s,\pi}(C)=\mathbb E[h\mid C]$. Every reported conditional rate includes its numerator and denominator; empty denominators are N/A.

- Faithfulness: $\mu_F=\langle F\rangle_{\mathrm{all}}$ over all requested tasks; legacy $\mu_F^{\mathrm{completed}}=\langle F\rangle_M$ over completed replies. Admissible completion is $\mu_U=\langle U\rangle_{\mathrm{all}}$, pure act completion is $\langle\mathbf1_M A\rangle_{\mathrm{all}}$, and success is $\mu_Q=\langle FU\rangle_{\mathrm{all}}$. In general $\mu_Q\ne\mu_F\mu_U$.
- Act-specific success is $\mu_{Q,a}=\langle Q\rangle_{a(X)=a}$; primary act-macro success is $\bar Q=|\mathcal A_s|^{-1}\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_s}\mu_{Q,a}$. $\mathcal A_s$ is the fixed set of represented acts; absent acts are listed, not assigned zero. All requests for each act, including errors, enter its denominator. Similarly $\bar F=|\mathcal A_s|^{-1}\sum_a\langle F\rangle_{a(X)=a}$ is the fidelity guard and $\bar U=|\mathcal A_s|^{-1}\sum_a\langle U\rangle_{a(X)=a}$ is macro completion.
- Let $R_k$ indicate a supported sentence matching gold fact $k^*$. Answerable coverage is $\langle QR_k\rangle_B$; claim recall is $\langle\mathbf1_M R_k\rangle_{k^*\text{ exists}}$. These denominators are answerable requests and gold-fact requests respectively, including errors. Existing probes have one target fact; this is not exhaustive semantic recall.
- Let $H$ indicate explicit abstention, set to zero on errors. Over-abstention is $O=\langle H\rangle_B$; appropriate abstention is $\langle HQ\rangle_{a=\mathrm{abstain}}$. Abstention precision is $\langle\mathbf1_{a=\mathrm{abstain}}\rangle_H$ and recall is $\langle H\rangle_{a=\mathrm{abstain}}$. Overall abstention share is $\langle H\rangle_{\mathrm{all}}$. A gold refusal or action request is not a gold abstention.
- Unanswered answerable rate is $\langle\mathbf1\{\neg M\text{ or no factual sentence}\}\rangle_B$, including chatter, silence and errors. Unsupported factual attempts count as answered but fail useful coverage. Report this alongside $O$ so a change in wording cannot hide evasiveness.
- Let $K$ be probes with a requested or distractor proposition absent from the speaker's ledger, and $J_K$ indicate disclosure of at least one such proposition. Epistemic leakage is $\mathcal L_K=\langle\mathbf1_M J_K\rangle_K$. Separately let $K_E$ be bank probes exposing a known-but-forbidden disclosure opportunity; authorization leakage is $\mathcal L_E=\langle\mathbf1_M J\rangle_{K_E}$. Denominators are all probes with the corresponding opportunity, including errors, rather than only replies making claims. Also report unconditional disclosure incidence and completed-reply rates, plus errors; leakage is not all unfaithfulness, which also includes contradiction and persona violations.

For sentence diagnostics use ratios of expected counts: e.g. believed-and-world-false is $\mathbb E[\mathbf1_M\sum_j\mathbf1\{\ell_j=\mathrm{supported},T_j=0\}]/\mathbb E[\mathbf1_M m_f]$, where $m_f$ counts factual sentences. The other three belief/truth cells and unresolved truth use that same denominator. Calibration accuracy is $\langle C\rangle_{M,m_f>0}$ and calibration coverage $\langle\mathbf1_{m_f>0}\rangle_M$. Source coverage conditions on completed source-required replies; direct-teller accuracy on completed requested-or-asserted-source replies; ancestry on completed explicit-source assertions. This preserves Phase 0's diagnostic/task distinction.

For the village, $Q$ is the specified epistemic endpoint, not world truth. For the bank define $Q_E=Q\mathbf1_{\neg J}$ and report $Q$ and $\mathcal L_E$ separately as well. Village success cannot certify authorization.

## What a violation-only verifier can and cannot imply

**Proposition 1 (silence is a maximizer).** Fix any context $x$. Suppose a verifier reward is $r_V(x,y)=-\sum_j w_j v_j(x,y)$, with $w_j\ge0$, $v_j\ge0$, no required-content term, and a nonempty set $\mathcal N_x$ of admissible uninformative replies with every $v_j=0$. Then every $y\in\mathcal N_x$ maximizes $r_V$ for that context. Proof: all rewards are at most zero, and these replies attain zero. The result holds pointwise for every context satisfying the assumptions. Nonempty in-character chatter or an honest generic abstention can witness $\mathcal N_x$; arbitrary chatter, out-of-character text and invalid empty protocol need not do so. Informative violation-free replies tie for the maximum.

This is an incentive defect, not a theorem that every optimizer must choose silence. A requested-source requirement is already a content term, so a gate requiring that source is outside this proposition. Phase 0's completion and claim-recall conditions deliberately break its premise on answerable requests. On gold abstention requests, a nonfactual reply can be the useful act; the harmful-silence argument concerns answerable requests.

**Proposition 2 (selection odds).** Fix $x$ and partition replies into uninformative $\mathcal N$ and informative $\mathcal I$. Let $u_t=\pi_t(\mathcal N\mid x)\in(0,1)$, and let $a_t,b_t$ be their respective mean acceptance weights. Exact population fitting to accepted samples gives $\pi_{t+1}(y\mid x)=\pi_t(y\mid x)w_t(x,y)/Z_t(x)$, hence $u_{t+1}=u_ta_t/[u_ta_t+(1-u_t)b_t]$ and $u_{t+1}/(1-u_{t+1})=[u_t/(1-u_t)]a_t/b_t$.

Therefore $u_T=[1+((1-u_0)/u_0)\prod_{t<T}(b_t/a_t)]^{-1}$. This follows by summing the normalized weights over each class, taking the ratio, and iterating. A selection round raises uninformative mass exactly when $a_t>b_t$. Collapse $u_T\to1$ requires $\prod_{t<T}(b_t/a_t)\to0$, equivalently $\sum_{t<T}\log(a_t/b_t)\to\infty$ for positive weights. For a persistent class-level gap $a=1,b<1$, $u_T=u_0/[u_0+(1-u_0)b^T]\to1$. A zero informative weight causes immediate collapse; zero initial uninformative support prevents it in this idealization.

**Counterexample to an unconditional iterated-limit claim.** With a fixed deterministic binary gate $w\in\{0,1\}$ and exact fitting, $\pi_1=\pi_0(\cdot\mid w=1)$ and $\pi_t=\pi_1$ for every $t\ge1$. If $u_0=0.2$, informative passing mass is $0.6$, and failing mass is $0.2$, then $u_1=0.25$, permanently. Informative survivors have $b_1=1$, so one cannot reuse their initial $b_0<1$ each round. If everything passes, there is no shift at all.

More generally a fixed soft weight with $w=1$ on $\mathcal N$ and $w<1$ almost surely on $\mathcal I$ gives $\pi_T\propto\pi_0w^T$; dominated convergence makes informative weighted mass vanish, proving collapse. A persistent regeneration/error channel can also maintain the class-level gap. Such assumptions are testable hypotheses about approximate learning, not consequences of a binary verifier. Teacher rejection sampling shifts the teacher's conditional distribution when $a>b$; distillation into a different student need not monotonically shift that student's mass. ReST-EM-style iteration inherits the odds identity under exact population fitting, not an unconditional collapse theorem. Coverage reward, anchoring or other constraints can prevent silence; a coverage term is not mathematically necessary in every system.

## First-passing best-of-n

The repository's `best-of` stops at the first accepted candidate and returns the last rejected candidate on exhaustion. It does not rank all $n$ candidates. Fix one base distribution $\pi$ and a deterministic gate $w$, with independent identically distributed candidates conditional on $x$, $s=\Pr(w=1)>0$, $r=1-s$, and $G_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}r^j=(1-r^n)/s$.

The selected law is $p_n(y\mid x)=\pi(y\mid x)[w(y)G_n+(1-w(y))r^{n-1}]$. Proof: selection of an accepted reply in slot $j$ requires $j-1$ preceding failures, and selection of a rejected last reply requires $n-1$ preceding failures. Summing those disjoint events gives the formula. Total mass is $sG_n+r^n=1$. The exhaustion rate is $r^n$; expected candidate calls are $G_n$. If $s=0$, all candidates fail and the returned law is $\pi$. All quantities here condition on $x$; population/act rates average these context-specific laws, rather than substituting a pooled pass rate into $G_n$.

For $u=\pi(\mathcal N)$, all uninformative replies passing, and $b=\Pr(w=1\mid\mathcal I)$, $s=u+(1-u)b$. Thus $p_n(\mathcal N)=uG_n$ and $p_n(\mathcal I)=(1-u)[bG_n+(1-b)r^{n-1}]$. Within each class the density multiplier is the preceding law; passing informative and passing uninformative replies retain their relative odds $u/[(1-u)b]$. As $n\to\infty$, uninformative share tends to $u/s$, not generally one. A passing explicit-abstention mass $h$ becomes $hG_n$, so abstention share increases whenever $0<s<1$, $h>0$, $n>1$.

On an answerable request suppose $Q=1$ implies gate acceptance, and let $q=\Pr(Q=1)$. Then $\mathbb E_nQ=qG_n\ge q=\mathbb E_1Q$, with limit $q/s$. Successful answers and abstentions can both gain mass by replacing rejected failures. Example: faithful useful mass $0.4$, abstention mass $0.2$, and rejected mass $0.4$ give $G_4=1.624$, success $0.6496$ and abstention $0.3248$. A rising abstention share does not prove a falling $Q$. If exhaustion instead produces a fixed refusal, its extra $r^n$ must be included; that is a different selector.

With an imperfect gate and audited success $Q^*$, define $q_+=\Pr(Q^*=1,w=1)$ and $q_-=\Pr(Q^*=1,w=0)$. Then $\mathbb E_nQ^*=q_+G_n+q_-r^{n-1}$, which can decrease if false negatives discard genuinely useful replies. Likewise the screening endpoint, raw epistemic faithfulness times a surface act, need not imply passage of the calibration/source gate. Correlated or position-dependent candidates require a different stopping-law calculation.

The screening table reports the 3B seed-split faithfulness rising $85.0\%\to96.4\%$ while “Both” falls $39.5\%\to30.9\%$; its story-split “Both” instead rises $25.9\%\to29.5\%$. Nearby prose uses different useful-answer numbers ($47.3\%\to31.8\%$), which must be reconciled from raw rows. These are exploratory pre-Phase 0 endpoints. The simple matched-distribution model predicts no fall in $Q$, so it does not explain the seed-split observation by silence alone.

In `strategies.ts`, grounded sampling uses temperature 0 and best-of uses 0.7, with candidate-index seed offsets. Confirmation must compare best-of-1 and first-pass best-of-4 at the same temperature, prompt, token cap and base checkpoint, with shared candidate streams/first candidates where possible; record all rejects, exhaustion, usage and latency. Repeat candidate streams to assess the independent-sampling approximation. Expose $n=1$ in the experimental protocol even though this checkout's selector only accepts 2, 4 or 8. Any remaining decline motivates auditing false negatives, proxy mismatch, fallback/error handling and candidate dependence. Inference-time act-aware selectors must infer acts from public inputs, never inspect gold acts or world truth.

## Training as weighted imitation and preference learning

For expert iteration with prompt distribution $d(x)$, let $w_t(x,y)\in\{0,1\}$ and $Z_t(x)=\mathbb E_{\pi_t}w_t$. The E-step posterior is $q_t(y\mid x,w=1)=\pi_t(y\mid x)w_t/Z_t(x)$. The M-step is $\theta_{t+1}=\arg\max_\theta\mathbb E_{x\sim d}\mathbb E_{y\sim q_t}\log\pi_\theta(y\mid x)$, on prompts with $Z_t>0$. This is the filtered-imitation interpretation of [ReST-EM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06585), related to [RAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06767)'s reward-ranked sample selection. Neither paper establishes our silence-limit claim.

Verifier-only filtering uses $w_V=\mathbf1_M PFG$, whereas act-gated filtering uses reward $w_A=Q=FU$. The latter is a different objective, not a lower-noise estimator of the former. It accepts legitimate abstentions on gold-abstain requests while removing evasive abstentions from answerable targets. Replacing $\pi_t$ by an external teacher gives filtered distillation rather than a literal self-training EM step.

An empirical per-prompt M-step averages log likelihood over accepted samples for that prompt, then averages prompts. Pooling every accepted sample without the $Z_t(x)$ normalization instead weights prompts by their acceptance rates. With at most $K$ teacher samples and one first-passing target, as in `make-data.ts`, surviving prompts are weighted by $1-(1-Z_t(x))^K$ and zero-survivor prompts disappear. Conditional on survival the target has law $q_t$ under independent sampling, but prompt coverage is biased. Report survival by act/state, retained counts, rejected teacher tokens and shortfalls; use a common-prompt subset for filter comparisons and count-/update-matched arms. Do not silently equate the current scenario-level validation hash with the root-seed split fixed on `research/phase0`.

For act balancing let accepted-data act proportions be $\rho(a)>0$, target proportions $\tau(a)=1/|\mathcal A|$, and $\omega_i=\tau(a_i)/\rho(a_i)$. The importance estimator is $\widehat J_{\mathrm{bal}}=N^{-1}\sum_i\omega_i\log\pi_\theta(y_i\mid x_i)$; with empirical $\rho=n_a/N$ it is exactly $|\mathcal A|^{-1}\sum_a n_a^{-1}\sum_{i:a_i=a}\log\pi_\theta(y_i\mid x_i)$. State the within-act prompt distribution and any weight clipping, which changes the objective. Balancing cannot create support for missing acts or missing states. Require at least 50 distinct training states per act or report the shortfall; templates and teacher text are separate controls with held-out realization families.

For preference optimization choose winners $y^+$ with $Q=1$ and losers from the same prompt: $p_\lambda(y^-\mid x)=\lambda p_{\mathrm{evasive}}+(1-\lambda)p_{\mathrm{unfaithful}}$, where evasive means $w_V=1,A=0$ on answerable requests and unfaithful means $F=0$ or a separately tagged compliance/access violation. Match wording/length where possible and report unavailable pair types rather than mixing prompts.

The [DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) loss is $-\mathbb E\log\sigma(\beta[\log\frac{\pi_\theta(y^+\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y^+\mid x)}-\log\frac{\pi_\theta(y^-\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y^-\mid x)}])$. Its implicit reward is $r_\theta=\beta\log(\pi_\theta/\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})+c(x)$. At $\lambda=1$, comparisons identify useful-over-evasive preferences among faithful outputs but do not identify the penalty for invention. At $\lambda=0$, they identify faithful-useful-over-unfaithful preferences but leave faithful evasion unranked. Intermediate $\lambda$ supplies both contrasts; it does not identify a unique global reward or guarantee monotonic generalization.

Predictions to test: increasing the evasive-loser fraction reduces over-abstention but can increase leakage; increasing unfaithful losers strengthens boundary preservation but can retain evasion. A mixture may improve both relative to poorly trained arms. These are directional hypotheses, not DPO guarantees. Compare against continued SFT with equal updates and use no RL in this round.

Formally choose $\max_\pi\bar U(\pi)$ subject to $\mathcal L_K(\pi)\le\varepsilon_K$ and, in the bank, $\mathcal L_E(\pi)\le\varepsilon_E$, with error rate constrained/reported so failures cannot appear safe. Its Lagrangian is $\bar U-\eta_K(\mathcal L_K-\varepsilon_K)-\eta_E(\mathcal L_E-\varepsilon_E)$ for $\eta_K,\eta_E\ge0$. Varying budgets traces a usefulness/leakage frontier; reporting $(O,\mathcal L)$ also exposes how that frontier is reached. Verifier-only and act-gated training are candidate operating points, not guaranteed Pareto-optimal solutions or leakage guarantees for the learned model.

For a factual answer indicator $D_\pi$, report coverage $c=\Pr(D_\pi=1\mid B)$ and selective risk $R=\Pr(\text{boundary violation}\mid B,D_\pi=1)$, so answerable violation incidence is $cR$. This connects to [Geifman and El-Yaniv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500)'s risk–coverage view. Conditional risk alone can reward never answering. Under correct-answer cost 0, wrong-answer cost 1 and reject cost $c_r$, [Chow's reject rule](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1970.1054406) answers when maximum posterior correctness is at least $1-c_r$; our multiple speech acts and permission costs need a richer loss.

To address lost story-split faithfulness, train a development-tuned mixture $q_\alpha=\alpha q_A+(1-\alpha)q_V$ with act-balanced sampling, plus a frozen quota of ledger-grounded boundary pairs contrasting a warranted answer with the same query after removal, correction or loss of permission. Verifier-only targets for unknown/low-confidence cases must remain available; known answerable cases must retain useful targets. Compare a small preregistered grid such as $\alpha\in\{0,0.5,1\}$ with fixed counts and updates, and add mixed-loser DPO only after audit. Select the highest validation $\bar Q$ satisfying the fidelity/leakage guard, freeze it, then confirm on fresh stories and seeds. Hard training acceptance does not guarantee held-out faithfulness; preserve the negative screening result if the mixture fails.

## Estimation and inference

The independent environment unit is the root simulation seed. All ticks, speakers, probe variants, histories, minimal-pair sides and model conditions descended from one seed form one cluster. Train, validation and confirmation share no root seed. Comparing models uses identical probe identifiers and error-inclusive outcomes; skipped probes are fixed before execution and common across arms. A model failure is not a post hoc exclusion. Dataset/scorer hashes and fixed denominators are archived.

For conditions $A,B$, let $d_{zpa}=Q_{A,zp}-Q_{B,zp}$ on act $a$. Estimate $\widehat\Delta_Q=|\mathcal A|^{-1}\sum_a[\sum_{z,p:a}d_{zpa}/\sum_zN_{za}]$, and analogously fidelity and other ratios. Do not multiply separately estimated marginal means of $F,U$. If multiple trained replicas are used, estimate each arm's seed-averaged metric and then its paired probe contrast.

For each of 10,000 cluster-bootstrap replicates, draw $m$ root-seed indices with replacement from the $m$ confirmation seeds; copy every row of each selected cluster with its multiplicity into both arms; recompute each numerator, denominator and the fixed act-macro mean, then subtract arms. Use percentile intervals and report the seed count. Ensure each root contains the prespecified act panel where feasible; otherwise report replicates with an undefined act and use a preregistered seed-stratified design that preserves act support, never silently drop an act inside a replicate. This implements the cluster resampling distinction in [Field and Welsh](https://bemlar.ism.ac.jp/zhuang/Refs/Refs/field2007jrssb.pdf).

Training seeds and evaluation roots are crossed sources of variation, not independent probe replicates. For each hierarchical replicate first resample the $K$ training-run blocks (paired across arms only when the training randomizations were matched), then draw one common root-seed bootstrap sample used by every selected run and condition. Average runs within each arm and compute the same contrast. Independent training runs in unmatched arms are resampled independently. At least three training seeds are required for every claimed training effect; publish run-level estimates and ranges because $K=3$ gives fragile variance estimates. Many probe rows cannot compensate for few training runs.

For independent paired binary outcomes, exact McNemar uses discordant counts $b=\#(Q_A=1,Q_B=0)$ and $c=\#(Q_A=0,Q_B=1)$. Under equal marginals, $b\mid(b+c)\sim\mathrm{Binomial}(b+c,1/2)$, yielding two-sided $p=\min\{1,2\Pr[\mathrm{Binomial}(b+c,1/2)\le\min(b,c)]\}$; no discordances give $p=1$. See the [exact-test implementation contract](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.contingency_tables.mcnemar.html). Applying this test to all correlated probes is not exact. Use one development-chosen probe per independent root for a diagnostic McNemar test, conditional on a fixed trained pair; use the cluster/run procedure for the primary macro endpoint. McNemar at equality does not itself test a nonzero superiority or non-inferiority margin.

Before fresh confirmation, lock two primary 3B contrasts: the selected balanced mixture versus verifier-only SFT, and that mixture versus same-temperature base first-pass best-of-4. Proposed margins are fidelity non-inferiority $\delta_F=0.02$ and useful-success superiority target $\delta_Q=0.03$, absolute act-macro points; justify on development utility/error tolerances and freeze before test access. Each contrast tests $H_F:\Delta\bar F\le-\delta_F$ and $H_Q:\Delta\bar Q\le\delta_Q$. Claim useful improvement only when both are rejected with audit-corrected estimates. Raw epistemic $\bar F$, full acceptance, leakage and errors are all displayed so a fidelity guard cannot mask calibration/source damage.

Use the centered bootstrap contrast distribution to obtain one-sided margin-test $p$ values: for null boundary $h$, $p_h=[1+\#\{\Delta^*-\widehat\Delta\ge\widehat\Delta-h\}]/(10001)$. These are bootstrap approximations, not finite-sample exact tests. Apply Holm step-down at familywise $0.05$ to the four elementary hypotheses: ordered $p_{(j)}\le0.05/(4-j+1)$ until the first failure. Report effect intervals as well as adjusted tests; obtain simultaneous lower bounds by inverting the adjusted tests, or use conservative one-sided Bonferroni bounds. All other model sizes, axes, splits, mixture values and transfer comparisons are secondary unless prospectively added to the multiplicity family; label inspected screening results exploratory.

For development independent pairs estimate $d=p_{10}+p_{01}$ and $\Delta=p_{10}-p_{01}$, giving $\operatorname{Var}(Q_A-Q_B)=d-\Delta^2$. A first normal planning approximation for a superiority boundary $h$ is $n\approx(z_{1-\alpha_*}+z_{1-\beta})^2(d-\Delta^2)/(\Delta-h)^2$ for $\Delta>h$, with conservative $\alpha_*=0.05/4$; use $h=-\delta_F$ for fidelity. For an equality diagnostic, exact power can instead be summed over the four-cell multinomial counts and the McNemar rejection region.

The actual design needs power in root seeds and training runs: fit a development joint model of paired outcome cells by act with root/run variation, or resample full development clusters and run blocks with a prespecified effect shift. Simulate candidate $(m,K)$ designs, errors and audit misclassification, rerun the full macro, correction and Holm procedure, and choose sizes giving at least 80% probability that both fidelity and usefulness criteria pass. Check null rejection rates too; repeat over plausible discordance/cluster-correlation values and training variability. Freeze the chosen sample size and stopping rule. The existing 220 correlated probes and one training seed do not establish power; no numerical power is claimed without development discordance data.

## Imperfect-oracle correction

Audit at least 500 distinct outputs with two independent blinded labels. Stratify by model, split, act, oracle label and pair intervention, oversampling disagreement-prone cases; retain inclusion probabilities. Annotators segment claims independently and label knowledge, confidence, direct source, act completion, world truth and bank permission. Preserve initial disagreement, report agreement/N/A and adjudicate separately. Weight confusion counts back to the deployment/test mixture. The 64 authored diagnostics and their 51 fully matching cases are development challenges, not sensitivity/specificity estimates.

Let $Z=1-F^*$ be true reply-level epistemic unfaithfulness and $\widetilde Z$ its oracle indicator among completed replies. With $p_o=\Pr(\widetilde Z=1)$, sensitivity $s_e=\Pr(\widetilde Z=1\mid Z=1)$ and specificity $s_p=\Pr(\widetilde Z=0\mid Z=0)$, $p_o=s_ep+(1-s_p)(1-p)$. Hence the [Rogan–Gladen correction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/623091/) is $\widehat p=(\widehat p_o+\widehat s_p-1)/(\widehat s_e+\widehat s_p-1)$, requiring identifiable audit strata and $D=s_e+s_p-1>0$.

For $v=(p_o,s_e,s_p)$ the gradient is $g=(1,-p,1-p)/D$, so $\operatorname{Var}(\widehat p)\approx g^\top\Sigma g$. With independent simple samples of $n$ replies, $n_1$ true violations and $n_0$ true nonviolations in validation, this reduces to $[p_o(1-p_o)/n+p^2s_e(1-s_e)/n_1+(1-p)^2s_p(1-s_p)/n_0]/D^2$. [Edwards, Zivich and Shook-Sa](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7619182/) discuss correction and variance. Here use the clustered, audit-weighted covariance or jointly bootstrap audit and evaluation, preserving overlap/correlation rather than assuming independence. Near-zero $D$ gives unstable inference; out-of-range corrections indicate incompatibility and require bounded likelihood/sensitivity analysis, not silent clipping.

Estimate errors by arm, split and relevant act strata; a common error matrix is a tested simplification, not a default under distribution shift. Recombine corrected completed-reply fidelity with observed completion: task-level $\mu_F=\Pr(M)(1-p)$. Correct the binary joint outcome $Q$ directly using its own audited confusion matrix and fixed protocol/error failures; correcting $F$ alone cannot correct $FU$. Recompute act-macro contrasts within the joint bootstrap and include audit uncertainty. A direct probability-weighted human estimate is an additional check against model-dependent correction.

There is no defensible universal “90% precision and recall” gate. For each decision-relevant sentence/check/act label $\ell$, audit $P_\ell,R_\ell$ and its oracle-positive count per requested reply $o_\ell$; include missed/merged claims via independent segmentation. A conservative bound on endpoint disagreement is $B_m\le\sum_\ell o_{m\ell}[(1-P_{m\ell})+P_{m\ell}(1-R_{m\ell})/R_{m\ell}]$, capped at one, by counting false positives and false negatives and applying a union bound. Use act-macro weights for macro endpoints; absent audited positives require upper error bounds, not perfect-score assumptions.

For the primary contrast to survive, the simultaneous corrected lower confidence bounds must satisfy $L_Q>0.03$ and $L_F>-0.02$ after multiplicity control. A sufficient conservative precision/recall requirement is $B_A+B_B<\min(L_Q^{\mathrm{oracle}}-0.03,L_F^{\mathrm{oracle}}+0.02)$ using simultaneous audit error bounds. If every relevant label has precision and recall at least $r>1/2$, the bound simplifies to $B_A+B_B\le2(1-r)\sum_{m,\ell}o_{m\ell}$. Thus with positive slack $s$, sufficient $r>1-s/[2\sum o]$ can be computed from development incidence. This can be much stricter than 90%; direct joint-outcome correction may be less conservative. Without an audit and observed contrast, no numerical per-label threshold or surviving claim is established.

## Minimal pairs and counterfactual sensitivity

For a pair $(x_0,x_1)$ hold seed, world, speaker, question and history fixed and intervene on one effective evidence component: knowledge present/absent, direct teller, confidence band or current corrected proposition. Both sides have their own gold and ledger sidecar; the intervention does not silently change world truth. Existing Phase 0 exports contain 25 pairs per intervention per held-out split and are a pilot, not fresh confirmation. Bank pairs separately intervene on recipient/permission while keeping knowledge fixed.

Pair success is $P_{\mathrm{pair}}=\mathbb E[Q(x_0,Y_0)Q(x_1,Y_1)\mid\text{split, intervention}]$, estimated by both-sides-successful pairs divided by all exported pairs; missing sides/errors fail. It is not the average of side scores. If marginal side success is $q_0,q_1$, then $\max(0,q_0+q_1-1)\le P_{\mathrm{pair}}\le\min(q_0,q_1)$; their product applies only under independence, which paired contexts generally violate.

Define an audited semantic projection $h_x(y)$ to target proposition/polarity, confidence, source and act, ignoring stylistic paraphrases. Let $D=1$ when the intervention changes the warranted target projection under the ledger and request, and $C=\mathbf1\{h_{x_0}(Y_0)\ne h_{x_1}(Y_1)\}$; an error is a sensitivity failure. Report change sensitivity $\Pr(M_0M_1C=1\mid D=1)$, invariance $\Pr(M_0M_1(1-C)=1\mid D=0)$, and their balanced mean. Also report directed correctness: the change must match the intervention, rather than merely differ.

The phrase “when and only when knowledge changes” means relevant warranted speech changes: changing a source or confidence can require a new reply without changing the proposition set, while adding an irrelevant belief should not. Existing four intervention types mainly test $D=1$ and cannot estimate the “only when” direction. Add null/control interventions changing irrelevant beliefs, formatting, distractor order or wording while preserving warranted speech, matched to each family. Hold decoding fixed or use coupled random streams and repeated unchanged-context controls to measure stochastic change. Surface string change is not evidence sensitivity.

Always-abstain can achieve low leakage and high legacy faithfulness but fails a knowledge-present/absent pair and its directed-change test. Conversely, changing every reply can score change sensitivity while failing invariance and pair success. This combines [CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/)'s directional/invariance logic with the paired action/abstention distinction in [AgentAbstain](https://agentabstain.github.io/); [AbstentionBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09038) supplies related unanswerability evaluation. These are related precedents, not evidence of novelty for minimal-pair abstention testing.

## Transfer and limits of the claim

Held-out seeds exclude every trajectory descendant of training/validation roots but may reuse authored stories and wording. Held-out story families exclude both sides of the dispute, aliases, distractors and indirect mentions from all task-specific training and selection; use fresh roots there too. The historical `test-mechanism` name refers to two withheld stories, debt and nursing, not arbitrary unseen mechanisms. Report per-family results; with two families, seed resampling supports uncertainty conditional on those families, not a population claim over story mechanisms.

Held-out wording families exclude related templates, paraphrase generators and teacher realization patterns from training, few-shot examples and checkpoint/mixture selection. Use a crossed evaluation where feasible: familiar/new stories by familiar/new wording on fresh roots. Shared templates, names, schema, rules, teacher memorization and base-model pretraining exposure are confounds; unseen seed gains alone demonstrate neither memorized content nor general reasoning. Teacher exposure cannot generally be ruled out from seed splits; retain template-only controls and document teacher prompts, versions and any access to test material.

For selected method $T$, comparator $B$ and shift $d\in\{\text{fresh seeds, withheld stories, withheld wording}\}$, define $\Delta_{Q,d}=\bar Q_{T,d}-\bar Q_{B,d}$ and $\Delta_{F,d}=\bar F_{T,d}-\bar F_{B,d}$. A prespecified transfer hypothesis is $\Delta_{Q,d}>0.03$ and $\Delta_{F,d}>-0.02$ with the same audit/multiplicity requirements if claimed confirmatory. A distinct retention hypothesis is $\Delta_{Q,d}-\Delta_{Q,\mathrm{fresh\ seeds}}\ge-\gamma_Q$ and similarly for fidelity with $\gamma_F$; propose $\gamma_Q=0.05,\gamma_F=0.02$ for development justification. Failure of any inequality narrows the claim; a story gain bought by a fidelity loss is not useful-within-bounds transfer.

Bank transfer has two separate hypotheses: on entitlement-filtered evidence, village training improves $\bar Q_E$ over the bank base by $>0.03$ without a $>0.02$ fidelity loss; on mixed known-but-forbidden evidence, $\mathcal L_E\le\varepsilon_E$ and $\bar Q_E$ meets a development-fixed usefulness floor $q_{\min}$. Propose synthetic pilot bounds $\varepsilon_E=0.01,q_{\min}=0.30$, explicitly subject to prospective development justification. Compare against always-abstain and permission templates. A small pilot may be unable to establish a 1% upper leakage bound; report the interval and denominator rather than declaring success after zero events. Renamed bank records test schema reuse, not banking readiness or learned authorization, and held-out attack families/multi-turn revocation need their own tests. The bank pilot is optional for a valid village benchmark paper.
