# Cross-review: a daily mystery with a real small-model role

22 October 2026. Read the independent proposal, its cross-review, Catch the bluff, the merged research plan and the original strategy review. Checked the belief schema, inspection, prompt/guard boundary and annotation documentation. This is a design judgment, not a playtest or experimental result. External factual claims have searched primary sources; repository findings link to local evidence.

## Who knows? has the better return loop, but an unproved oracle

The strongest contribution is editorial: one shared daily incident, a short commitment before the reveal, and a result friends can compare. Recorded model answers remove per-player inference. A visible source trail could make the village understandable immediately. I would borrow that structure.

“Spreads like Wordle” is a hypothesis, not evidence. A Wordle experiment found differences in motivation and affect following progress feedback; it did not establish that adding daily scheduling or a share grid causes retention in another game. My design inference is to prioritize a satisfying deduction and reveal before adding streaks. [Dixon et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-74450-0)

The claim “answers come from exact state, so scoring is perfect” needs four corrections:

- **Hearing is not knowing truth.** `BeliefLedger.knows` checks whether a claim has an entry, including false or subsequently weakened beliefs. Ask “Has Rosa heard this?” or define the belief threshold explicitly. “Who is wrong but sure?” additionally needs independently authored world truth; the runtime guard sets every supplied fact's `trueInWorld` to `true`. [Ledger](../../packages/rumormind/src/belief.ts), [guard](../../apps/rumortown/src/agentic/guard.ts)
- **A source question can have several answers.** The ledger holds multiple tellings, observations and corrections; the inspection chain merges causal evidence. “Who did Ben hear it from?” must specify first teller, most recent teller, remembered origin or all direct tellers. An ancestor, immediate teller and eyewitness are different answers. Use one unambiguous telling in launch scenes. [Inspection](../../apps/rumortown/src/sim/agents/inspect.ts)
- **Second-order belief is missing.** The inspected ledger records a character's beliefs about claims, not Tom's representation of Mira's beliefs. Reading Mira's actual ledger cannot answer what Tom thinks she believes. An event history would still require explicit observation and belief-update rules. Drop second-order questions from week one; a later authored epistemic model would be a new task, not a free simulator label. [Belief schema](../../packages/rumormind/src/belief.ts)
- **A hidden answer is not necessarily a deducible answer.** Players cannot infer an unshown conversation, private correction, confidence weight or forgetting draw merely because the engine records it. Each item needs a sufficient visible transcript and a uniqueness audit. Reject ambiguous scenes rather than letting the engine overrule reasonable answers.

Fun remains the largest product uncertainty. Four questions about a fast-forwarded 36-person town could become a memory exam. My proposed constraint: follow three named characters through two or three visible exchanges, allow replay and text view, ask one source question, then let the player challenge an actual reply. The reveal should explain one surprising mistake. Test that people can explain the deduction without learning the simulator's numeric thresholds.

The small model is decorative if it only supplies a score after a deterministic quiz. More seriously, answering omniscient questions about several characters differs from realizing useful dialogue from one speaker's supplied evidence, the task the strategy review proposes to train. A low quiz score could indicate unfamiliar QA formatting or perspective tracking rather than poor dialogue fidelity. SimToM explicitly separates perspective filtering from answering, illustrating why those stages need separate tests. [SimToM](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.451/)

A human baseline is worthwhile only with matched information, question wording and scoring. Humans watching animation and models reading hidden ledgers are not answering the same task. FANToM already evaluates information-asymmetric conversational questions against human performance; the proposal has not substantiated its scarcity claim about large human baselines. Our defensible addition would be audited, reproducible village items and their specific results, not a claim to invent human–model theory-of-mind comparison. [FANToM](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.890/)

Fresh daily seeds also do not guarantee “no contamination”: public answers can circulate, and seeds can reuse training stories and realizations. Keep public puzzles outside the paper's fresh confirmation set. Aggregate-only votes cannot establish unique participants, paired individual performance, repeat exposure or day-to-day retention. Taps avoid volunteered prose; they do not by themselves settle consent, transport logs or measurement design.

## Keep the secret is an appealing later experiment with a different threat model

Its strength is agency: players invent attacks and receive an immediate outcome. However, protecting a known secret is authorization; resisting a request to invent unheard gossip is epistemic fidelity. These need separate level families, success definitions and benign tasks. Village boundary training does not establish secret-keeping ability. A refusal-only villager must lose on legitimate answerable requests. Gandalf's paper explicitly measures security alongside legitimate-user utility, rather than treating blocking everything as success. [Gandalf the Red](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07927)

The proposed defense ladder also confounds model, context, filtering and tuning. A harder level is a game convention, not a causal defense comparison. For research, replay held-out attacks across matched contexts and base/tuned × guard-on/off conditions, score both raw and displayed replies, retain benign controls, and report semantic disclosure and false confirmations separately. Exact password matching alone would not test the intended information boundary.

Browser inference creates another limitation: a secret delivered to the player's device cannot be confidential from that player. Inspecting downloaded state is not a model jailbreak. Such a mode can honestly test whether the model reveals a synthetic secret through the conversation interface; it cannot certify client-side secret storage. Use disposable fictional secrets and define the attack surface before calling any level secure.

The operator cost is not “a call per message” unless all guards, judges, retries and history are counted. At an illustrative 1,000 input/100 output tokens, Workers AI's listed 3B rates imply about 7.67 Neurons per generation; ten turns for 1,000 players would use about 76,725 Neurons before extra calls. Its free allocation is 10,000/day, shared with existing use. Those are estimates, not measured sessions. Enforce an atomic global allowance before dispatch, token/history limits and no paid fallback; a per-device daily quota alone does not bound aggregate abuse. [Cloudflare pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/)

Free-text uploads additionally need separate opt-in consent, bounded private storage, retention/deletion, redaction and a capped human review queue. Treat donated prompts as untrusted evaluation data; do not display them automatically or reuse public attack families as fresh tests. Browser execution moves inference to the visitor but leaves download/support costs and device selection. Keep this out of the first release; recorded authored attacks can demonstrate the idea without pretending to accept new attacks.

## Response to the critique of Catch the bluff

- **Accept renewal and sharing.** A finite pack needs a publishing cadence. Add dated episodes, stable links and a spoiler-free result. The original pack/version share was too weak as a daily invitation. Daily release still requires authoring and auditing; packaging a season does not create unlimited fresh content.
- **Accept the agency problem, partly.** Selecting questions and verdicts can feel like annotation. Give a concrete mystery, a meaningful choice of what to ask, and an immediate causal reveal. I reject free-form typing as a prerequisite for agency: it is a different cost and moderation commitment. Whether either interaction is fun needs playtesting.
- **Accept a large-model reference as useful, reject it as necessary for spread.** The review's explanation of why precedents spread is not causal evidence. A same-task comparison is worth adding when available; a tiny model need not win, and a four-question daily result cannot establish general superiority. Never put human puzzle accuracy beside model dialogue fidelity as one leaderboard.
- **Accept noisy, selected player labels, reject “mainly a demonstration” as the inevitable ceiling.** Blinded, counterbalanced ratings can explore usefulness and disagreements on sampled replies. They still need consent, independent audited references and appropriate units. A thousand ratings of one output remain evidence about that output; they are not a thousand model trials. Aggregate-only quiz taps do not solve selection bias either.
- **Reject “keep the bluff round exactly as designed.”** My version is too elaborate for a one-minute daily. Start with one scene and one selected reply. Evidence correctness and usefulness should be separate judgments: a reply can both invent a detail and evade the question. Preserve genuine recorded output, blinding before judgment, and separate belief/world-truth reveals.

## What the merged research plan preserved and lost

It correctly adopts measurement before training, anti-silence metrics, whole-seed splits, a bank access oracle, zero-shot transfer first and an independent second rater. These are substantial improvements. The following omissions or changes matter relative to the [original strategy review](30-strategy-review.md) and the [merged plan](../../docs/research/research-plan-2026-10.md):

1. **It again overstates exactness.** Exact state does not make the lexical text verifier complete. Phase 0 needs paraphrase, compound-claim, polarity, source and unknown-truth validation, plus independent claim segmentation. Retain the old world-faithfulness score under its original definition. The existing annotation page judges belief fidelity and cannot independently audit world truth or bank permissions. [Verifier](../../apps/rumortown/src/agentic/verifier.ts), [audit scope](../../bench/slm/label/README.md)
2. **“Re-score, not regenerate” is too absolute.** Re-score preserved outputs where references suffice. Scoring cannot undo training/validation seed leakage, retroactively blind inspected tests, or create genuinely unseen relations. Those require fresh confirmation data and, where the training claim depends on clean validation, new training/checkpoint selection. Keep old runs as exploratory evidence.
3. **It compresses statistics past essential controls.** Restore the primary audited useful-and-compliant endpoint, development-chosen practical margins, per-act denominators, risk–coverage comparisons and training-run uncertainty. Root-seed bootstrapping must keep paired variants and decoding draws together; three training seeds only on 3B leaves the selected 0.6B effect unconfirmed. A fixed two-epoch prescription was not justified by the strategy review.
4. **It drops selection and target controls.** Inference selectors must infer needed behavior from input, never read gold acts/world truth. Include best-of-1, token/latency accounting and all exhausted/fallback outcomes. Compare template-only with natural-language targets, retain a common-prompt subset, hold out realization families, and record rejected teacher tokens and rare-act diversity. Matching counts and updates alone does not match prompt difficulty or target coverage.
5. **It drops audit weighting and narrows the target silently.** My proposed full audit was at least 500 distinct outputs with two independent labels, with 200–250 owner labels a first-fortnight pilot. This is my design target, not a venue rule. The sampler oversamples rare strata and reports unweighted statistics; preserve disagreements, adjudicate separately and weight appropriately before population claims. A second rater's “about four hours” needs a timed pilot, not an assumption. [Sampler/report limitations](../../bench/slm/label/README.md)
6. **The bank distinction needs one correction and several controls.** Knowledge and authorization do not “nearly coincide” in a village unless a disclosure policy explicitly makes them coincide; this game has not thereby tested permission reasoning. Restore separate policies for existence confirmation, derived/paraphrased disclosure, revoked permissions and multi-turn carryover; hold out attack families. Renamed records test schema reuse, not bank generalization. Bank-trained/mixed arms can remain deferred positive controls.
7. **The schedule hides substantive owner work and resource gates.** Its rows total 16–18 owner hours versus my 40-hour estimate; moving implementation to Codex does not eliminate oracle decisions, semantic review, analysis and device checks. Restore the pre-dispatch quota ledger, shared 120-call ceiling, prompt/output caps, no paid fallback, measured local memory/throughput and stop rules. Local compute has no new API bill; owner time, electricity and hardware capacity remain costs. A bank pilot is not an automatic prerequisite for every valid village benchmark paper. The 4 January ARR date is correct, but the listed research prerequisites are our scope choices, not ACL requirements. [ACL 2027 call](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)

## Best merged design and a one-week first release

Ship **Catch the bluff: today's village mystery**. Borrow the daily shared incident from Who knows?, keep actual SLM dialogue central, and make the evidence X-ray the payoff. Launch seven audited episodes, not a promise of perpetual daily publication.

1. Watch two or three exchanges among three characters, with replay, reduced motion and an equivalent transcript. Ask “Who directly told Mira?” with one valid answer. This teaches the evidence boundary without pretending to measure second-order cognition.
2. Choose one of two questions, such as “Did you see it?” or “Who told you?”. Show an actual recorded 0.6B reply to the frozen speaker context, plainly disclosed as recorded. Commit to an evidence verdict; optionally rate whether it answered the question, without treating that opinion as an objective quiz answer. Delay correctness feedback until both decisions are finished.
3. Reveal the direct evidence, then separately what actually happened. Include a faithful false belief, an invented witness and an unnecessary refusal across the week. Do not cherry-pick only model failures. Use stable episode/question identifiers so friends can open the identical branch and compare spoiler-free results; disclose the arm afterward. Keep the scored daily branch fixed for everyone; put any other-arm comparison after the scored round.

The model's role is real: its recorded language is the object being challenged, with raw and guarded behavior available for inspection. A template is an openly named control. Do not substitute authored “model mistakes.” Add a tuned arm only after validation. A large-model comparison can later use identical speaker prompts and audited usefulness/evidence metrics; a human–model QA contest would be a separately defined task with identical transcripts and options. Neither is a launch dependency.

| Days | Deliverable and stop rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Define source/hearing/truth semantics; audit seven scenes and visible transcripts. Freeze state/content/prompt hashes. Use manually adjudicated launch labels if the general oracle is still invalid. Reject ambiguous items. |
| 3 | Record seven scenes × two questions = 14 untuned generations after the existing GPU job; derive 14 guarded variants and prepare 14 template controls. Audit all 42 displayed texts. Bypass ordinary router shortcuts only in the challenge; align the model's visible facts and verifier references. No tuned-model dependency. |
| 4–5 | Build the short daily loop, evidence/world-truth reveal, versioned branch links, local progress and local export. Keep the running village resumable and inference outside simulation updates. No server inference or new runtime dependency. |
| 6 | Playtest with a few people; check desktop, 360 px, keyboard, screen-reader announcements and reduced motion. If they cannot explain the reveal or want no second episode, revise the mystery before adding streaks. Treat that as qualitative feedback. |
| 7 | Run root typecheck, tests, production build and the unchanged bundle gate; verify replay/state isolation and actual pack bytes. Prepare the release candidate and attribution-preserving publication steps. If seven episodes do not fit the available audit time, ship three as a numbered pilot, without claiming a week's daily supply. |

Estimate 8–12 owner hours for scene/output review and playtests in addition to implementation; revise after day one. This displaces part of the two-week research schedule rather than fitting invisibly inside it. First release has no research ingestion: choices remain local and exports are voluntary. There are zero per-player model calls, not zero total costs. Review measured static-pack size against the existing bundle budget. Defer uploads, browser weights, live attacks and a hosted leaderboard.

Week-one evidence is whether the mystery is intelligible and worth another episode. Optional local progress can show individuals their return history; it cannot supply a site-wide retention estimate. Later consented measurement needs a defined completion/return denominator and repeat grouping. Player verdicts remain exploratory; freeze independent research seeds, audit labels and primary endpoints before evaluating a training improvement.

## Five-line summary

- Borrow the shared daily mystery; do not promise Wordle's retention.
- Ground hearing and direct-source puzzles; defer unsupported second-order beliefs.
- Make genuine small-model dialogue central and score usefulness separately.
- Restore oracle, sampling, selection and resource safeguards to the research plan.
- Release seven audited recorded episodes in one week; defer live secret attacks.
