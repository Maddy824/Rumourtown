# Cross-review of the formal methods core

Reviewed [the independent formal core](../../docs/research/methods-formal.md), called D below, against [the Codex methods draft](35-methods-codex.md), called C. D's section numbers are its existing 1–9; C's unnumbered sections are identified by title. Verdict: adopt D's compact exposition after the following mathematical repairs; neither draft establishes a confirmed training or transfer effect.

## A. Correctness audit

1. **D §1, state and gold:** finite ledgers, direct sources and entitlement subsets are sound abstractions. Determinism makes authored labels reproducible, not semantically infallible or world truth necessarily binary; retain unknown reference truth, time/revision, recipient/access context and an independently audited textual oracle. Gold must specify target content, confidence and source as well as the act.
2. **D §1, outcomes:** the displayed binary definitions are well formed, but raw $F$ omits calibration, source and permission gates. Thus $Q=FU$ with bare act completion is not full compliant success and need not pass $R_F$. Keep pure act completion $A$ separate; use village $Q=R_FA$ with protocol/completion gates included in $R_F$, and bank $Q_E=Q\mathbf1[\text{no access violation}]$. Access checking must cover semantic disclosures, not just asserted record identifiers. $1-F$ is not leakage: contradiction/persona failures and known-but-forbidden disclosures differ.
3. **D §1, rates:** $Q(\pi)=\mathbb E Q$ is correct. Replace $|\mathcal G|$ and the sum's index by a fixed represented-act set $\mathcal G_s$ when acts are absent. The coverage formula is valid once target recall is defined substantively; $\mathbb E[N\mid\text{answerable}]$ measures nonfactual replies, including chatter, rather than explicit over-abstention alone. Appropriate abstention must require the abstention act, not merely its surface proxy. Include errors in task denominators and specify N/A for empty conditional denominators.
4. **D §1, truth cells:** four cells plus unresolved sum to one only with one common factual-unit denominator and exhaustive assignment, including contradicted, unmatched and mixed-claim sentences. State those rules. The legacy $\forall c\,\tau_w(c)=1$ must quantify over claims actually voiced, not all world claims; retain unknown truth rather than forcing it into false.
5. **Equation (1) and proposition 1:** the maximizer proof is correct if every member of the chosen silent set passes every gate. “No factual sentence” alone does not imply that premise: out-of-character chatter can fail $F$, and required-source/protocol checks can fail. Restrict to admissible in-character, violation-free nonfactual replies and explicitly assume a nonempty such set per context. Informative passing replies tie; silence is an optimum, not the inevitable optimizer.
6. **Equation (2):** correct pointwise in $x$ under exact population fitting, positive normalizer and $0<n_t<1$, with $p_t>0$ for finite odds. The update is $n_{t+1}=n_t/[n_t+(1-n_t)p_t]$. A pooled $n_t,p_t$ does not generally obey this identity under separate per-context normalization. With $p_t=0$ and $n_t>0$ collapse is immediate; with $n_0=0$ exact reweighting cannot create silent support.
7. **Proposition 2's limit:** a persistent bound $p_t\le\bar p<1$ proves the conditional geometric claim, but is incompatible with a fixed deterministic binary verifier and exact fitting whenever informative survivors exist: after one pass, $\pi_1=\pi_0(\cdot\mid R_F=1)$ and $p_1=1$, so further filtering is idempotent. Counterexample: silent/pass mass $.2$, informative/pass $.6$, informative/fail $.2$ gives silence $.25$ forever after round one. Replace unconditional collapse/causal claims with the odds identity plus an explicit changing-gate, soft-weight or regeneration assumption; approximate student distillation has no guaranteed monotonic shift.
8. **Equation (3):** at fixed $x$, write $a=n_0+(1-n_0)p>0$. Conditional on any pass, informative share $(1-n_0)p/a$ and silent share $n_0/a$ are correct. Equality also holds at $n_0=1$, omitted in D; the zero-pass case $a=0$ has no conditional law. “Abstention” here is the broader $N$ class unless explicit abstention is separately measured.
9. **The claimed $Q/a$ gain:** valid only if $Q=1\Rightarrow R_F=1$, which D's definitions do not ensure. Generally the pass-conditional success is $\mathbb E[QR_F]/a$, not $\mathbb E[Q]/a$. Example: mass $.2$ silent/pass, $.4$ useful/pass, $.4$ useful/fail due to calibration/source checks gives base raw $Q=.8$ but pass-conditional $Q=2/3$. Repair the endpoint or state the implication as an extra hypothesis.
10. **Finite first-pass selection:** D's conditioning omits exhaustion. Let $G_n=[1-(1-a)^n]/a$, $q_+=\Pr(Q=1,R_F=1)$ and $q_-=\Pr(Q=1,R_F=0)$. Returning the last candidate on all failures gives $\mathbb E Q_{\rm selected}=q_+G_n+q_-(1-a)^{n-1}$ and $\Pr(N_{\rm selected})=n_0G_n$. If success implies passage, gain is $G_n$, not $1/a$; $1/a$ is the conditional gain or infinite-$n$ limit. At $n=1$ nothing changes. Both useful and silent shares can rise by replacing rejects; average these context-specific laws rather than using a pooled pass rate.
11. **D §2, screening and repair:** the same-temperature best-of-1 control is necessary and already in C. D's 47.3%→31.8% prose conflicts with the screening table's seed-split “Both” 39.5%→30.9%; story-split “Both” rises 25.9%→29.5% ([local screening record](../../docs/research/finetune-study.md)). Reconcile raw rows before interpretation. Under corrected $R_Q=R_FA$, exact filtering projects onto supported successes if their mass is positive; it has many fixed points, undefined zero-success prompts and no held-out guarantee. Nonfactual refusals/actions can also be useful, so “only when gold is abstain” is false.
12. **D §3, SFT:** the weighted negative-log-likelihood formula is correct for pooled accepted data, up to a global positive normalization. It is not the fixed-prompt-distribution M-step without division by $Z(x)=\mathbb E_{\pi_T}w$: pooling weights prompts by $Z(x)$. Finite first-survivor collection instead weights prompt survival by $1-(1-Z(x))^K$. An external teacher gives filtered distillation. “Differ only in $w$” describes a proposed control, not proof that existing arms match counts, updates, support and lengths. [ReST-EM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06585) and [RAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06767) motivate filtering, not the claimed silence limit.
13. **D §3, balancing:** inverse kept-act counts give an empirical uniform act mean when every $n_g>0$, with the within-act distribution unchanged. Call this reweighting the accepted-data act distribution, not the unfiltered teacher distribution. Under independent within-act sampling its mean-loss variance is $|\mathcal G_s|^{-2}\sum_g\sigma_g^2/n_g$; “grows with $\max 1/n_g$” is a warning/bound, not a universal variance law. Zero counts cannot be repaired by weighting; targeted/template supplementation changes the data distribution and needs separate controls.
14. **Equation (4) and Lagrangian:** the constrained optimization is mathematically valid as a fidelity constraint, not a leakage budget. The full Lagrangian is $\mathbb E U-\lambda(\mathbb E[1-F]-\varepsilon)$; dropping the constant is harmless for optimizing the policy at fixed $\lambda$. Unfiltered SFT does not maximize $U$ and hence is not the $\lambda=0$ solution; verifier-only is not generally a $\lambda\to\infty$ solution with the usefulness term retained. Passing training targets do not guarantee even the fitted policy's training-distribution risk is zero. Use separate knowledge/access budgets and measured candidate operating points, without asserting Pareto optimality or strong duality.
15. **D §3, DPO:** the displayed loss is correct for $\beta>0$ and supported reference probabilities ([original DPO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290)). At evasive share $\lambda=0$, useful-faithful winners beat unfaithful losers but faithful evasion is unranked; proposition 1 cannot imply silence maximizes this pairwise objective. At $\lambda=1$, faithfulness among compared replies is held fixed and invention remains unranked; confident answering is not forced. An intermediate mixture dominating SFT or tracing the same frontier as SFT is a hypothesis. Same-prompt pairing cancels additive prompt reward constants, not all difficulty or length confounding.
16. **D §3, mixture endpoint:** as defined, $w_1=FU+R_F(1-U)=R_F+FU(1-\mathrm{cal}\,\mathrm{src})$, so it exceeds verifier-only weight on faithful/on-act replies failing a gate. Counterexample: $F=U=1$, calibration $=0$ gives $w_1=1,R_F=0$. Fix $R_Q=R_FU$ for pure act $U$, giving $w_\mu=R_F[U+\mu(1-U)]$ and exact endpoints. If keeping C's admissible $U$, use a separate pure-act $A$ to avoid notation collision. Match normalization/sampling as well as weights when claiming equivalence to an actual arm.
17. **D §3, restraint interpretation:** the verifier-only-minus-act set is indeed $\{R_F=1,U=0\}$ even under D's original definitions; without nested gates, what fails is reconstructing verifier-only as the act set plus this residual. Correct abstentions already present can still be upweighted, so “adding abstentions changes nothing” is too strong. Their reported 60% share, the claim extra targets are mostly restraint, and availability of both loser types in 12,800 samples require a pool audit. A 19.6-point fidelity difference does not identify which targets caused transfer. Neither $\mu$ nor $\lambda$ is guaranteed to trace an identical or monotone frontier; balancing can also change mixture endpoints.
18. **D §4, inference:** a mean paired difference estimates micro $\Delta$; the primary macro endpoint needs a mean of within-act paired means. Root-cluster bootstrap with all descendants retained is appropriate; 2,000 percentile replicates are an approximation. Exact McNemar assumes independent pairs and tests equality of marginal binary rates, so pooled correlated probes give no exact test and cannot test the stated nonzero macro margins. Shared evaluation roots and training runs are crossed: resample run blocks, then one common root sample across selected runs/arms, rather than independent roots within runs; preserve genuine run matching only.
19. **D §4, decisions:** prospective development selection is sound, but unadjusted 95% lower limits plus an unexplained Holm step do not define one coherent rejection rule. Define four elementary margin hypotheses and adjusted one-sided tests or simultaneous lower bounds; freeze comparator, macro/micro fidelity guard and margins. D proposes .05/−.03 whereas C proposes .03/−.02 with different comparators: choose and justify one family prospectively, not whichever later passes. Neither draft's provisional numbers are established tolerances.
20. **D §4, power:** $\operatorname{Var}(Q_1-Q_0)=d-\Delta^2$ gives the reasonable large-sample equality-test approximation $n\approx[z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt d+z_{1-\beta}\sqrt{d-\Delta^2}]^2/\Delta^2$, replacing random discordance by its planning value; require $0<|\Delta|\le d\le1$, adequate discordances and integer rounding. It is not exact McNemar power or power for a .05 superiority boundary, Holm-adjusted macro success plus fidelity, audit error or multiple trained runs. $1+(m-1)\rho$ is valid for equal-sized exchangeable clusters with $\rho$ for the paired differences, not an arbitrary outcome ICC; unequal sizes/macro weights need a different calculation. Use C's joint root/run/audit design simulation for actual confirmation.
21. **D §5, correction:** $r_o=sr+(1-t)(1-r)$ and $\hat r=(\hat r_o+\hat t-1)/(\hat s+\hat t-1)$ are correct for transportable binary error rates and $D_0=s+t-1\ne0$; orient the classifier so $D_0>0$. Near-zero $D_0$ is unstable; incompatible rates need bounded estimation/sensitivity analysis. Correct the audited joint endpoint $Q$ separately: correcting $F$ alone cannot correct $FU$, and per-sentence corrections cannot simply be multiplied into reply success.
22. **D §5, variance:** its first term is correct for known $s,t$, but “terms in variances” omits necessary covariance. With $v=(r_o,s,t)$, $g=(1,-r,1-r)/D_0$, use $g^\top\Sigma g$. For independent evaluation/audit estimates this is $[\operatorname{Var}(\hat r_o)+r^2\operatorname{Var}(\hat s)+(1-r)^2\operatorname{Var}(\hat t)]/D_0^2$; otherwise include all cross terms via the weighted joint cluster/audit bootstrap. These formulas follow direct differentiation; the original correction is [Rogan–Gladen](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.aje.a112510).
23. **D §5, scaling and audit size:** .05/.6=.0833 and interval scaling $1/.6$ hold only for a common known error matrix; estimated/differential matrices add uncertainty and can alter contrasts. No universal SE<.03 follows from that example. Independent audit class counts must satisfy approximately $n_1>s(1-s)/.03^2$ and $n_0>t(1-t)/.03^2$; worst case needs about 278 of each, before clustering/weights. Five hundred outputs and two raters do not guarantee either class count or precision; two labels on one output are not two independent outputs.
24. **D §6, pair algebra:** $Q_2=QQ'$ is correct per pair; for marginal success $q,q'$, $\max(0,q+q'-1)\le\mathbb E Q_2\le\min(q,q')$. The covariance claim is wrong: $\mathbb E Q_2=qq'+\operatorname{Cov}(Q,Q')$, and error covariance equals success covariance. Perfectly correlated failures with both marginals .5 give joint success .5, above .25. Only negative covariance puts joint success below the product; nonzero correlation alone gives no direction.
25. **D §6, sensitivity:** literal biconditional $\sigma$ gives always-abstain score 1 when an intervention leaves $B_a$ unchanged, so “zero everywhere” fails on null controls. Changes in source/confidence need not change the claim's proposition, and irrelevant ledger changes need not change warranted speech. Use C's semantic target projection, warranted-change indicator, directed change and separate invariance controls, controlling decoding variation. Always-abstain fails an answerable pair only when completion substantively requires its target content; not every nonfactual legitimate act is abstention.
26. **D §7, transfer:** the inequalities are valid proposed hypotheses, subject to audit, selection and multiplicity controls. Seeds/stories/wording are shift axes, not mathematically nested or guaranteed difficulty ordered; cross story and wording conditions. Seed-only improvement proves neither memorization nor skill; story/wording gains support a bounded transfer claim, not general reasoning or absence of teacher exposure. Two withheld families support inference conditional on those families, not a population of mechanisms.
27. **D §8, energy:** $J_{\rm vua}=\sum E_j/\sum Q_j$ is valid when the numerator includes all attempts but the denominator counts one final success per requested task, not multiple successful retries. Zero success gives undefined/infinite cost; correct gold abstentions do earn $Q$, contrary to “abstains get no credit.” $N>S/(e_h-e_l)$ with $e_l<e_h$ is correct for fixed comparable per-task costs and differential setup $S$; comparisons per useful answer also need matched success or $e_l/q_l$ versus $e_h/q_h$. Keep this in the deployment appendix/paper.
28. **D §9 and evidence status:** the cited literature categories are appropriate precedents, not proofs of D's new propositions. Link and verify the final bibliography. Screening percentages are exploratory proxies from one training run; D's causal “restraint transferred” and “the filter, not the model” statements exceed the evidence. C already records these limits; retain them in the merge.

## B. What D adds and C should adopt

- D's concise nine-part narrative and explicit numbered equations give the main text a clearer spine; move C's lengthy operational details to the protocol appendix.
- Adopt the corrected residual-target weight $R_F[U+\mu(1-U)]$, which makes the inclusion of faithful off-act targets explicit. C instead proposes a mixture of normalized full datasets; the parameterizations are not interchangeable, especially after act balancing.
- Keep D's conditional-on-a-pass explanation beside C's finite selector law: it makes the simultaneous rise in successful answers and silence easy to see. C already has the actual law and prediction; this is an exposition improvement, not a missing result.
- Adopt the independent-pair McNemar planning formula as a labeled diagnostic approximation and D's complete energy-accounting paragraph for a deployment appendix. C already has stronger confirmation power planning but no energy section.

## C. What C adds and D should adopt

- C specifies errors/protocol gates, substantive targets, distinct epistemic/access leakage, explicit abstention versus nonfactual replies, world-truth unknowns and fixed denominators. It already supplies the binary-filter counterexample, general odds condition, finite selector/exhaustion law and false-negative case.
- C distinguishes per-prompt fitting from pooled/finite-survival prompt weighting; it specifies support/count/update controls, balanced estimators, boundary-pair supplementation and partial identification by typed DPO comparisons.
- C supplies crossed training/root resampling, independent-pair limits of McNemar, margin/multiplicity tests and simulated joint power. Its weighted blinded audit includes full delta covariance, direct joint-$Q$ correction and error bounds tied to decision slack, rather than an unsupported universal audit size.
- C defines pair bounds, semantic directed change/invariance/null interventions, crossed transfer, retention hypotheses, family-level limits and a separate bank permission pilot. Its prerequisite list keeps screening, proposed controls and completed evidence distinct.

## Five-line summary

Silence can maximize a violation-only reward; exact fixed binary filtering does not imply iterative collapse.  
Best-of-n raises success only under stated gate assumptions, with finite gain $G_n$ rather than $1/a$.  
Repair the mixture endpoint, pair-correlation claim, audit variance and scope of the power approximation.  
Use D's compact structure and residual mixture with C's measurement, design and inference safeguards.  
Freeze one audited, preregistered protocol before claiming useful transfer within knowledge or permission bounds.
