## The problem

The study is currently engineering: filter teacher samples with a verifier, fine-tune, measure. For a top venue it needs a formal core. Write it, as the methods section of the paper would read, in plain Markdown with inline math.

## What to produce

1. **Formal setup.** World state, per-agent belief ledgers with confidence bands and direct sources, entitlement (what may be disclosed to this recipient; equal to the ledger in the village, a subset in the bank), the context given to the policy (persona, rendered ledger, distractors, history, utterance), the gold speech act, the policy, and the verifier as a function from (context, reply) to sentence labels plus calibration and source checks. Define epistemic faithfulness F, act completion U, the primary outcome Q = F·U, leakage, over-abstention, and the act-macro average, each as an expectation with its conditioning set and denominator, consistent with the Phase 0 definitions.
2. **The silence result, stated and proved.** Make precise and prove: for any verifier that scores only violations and never requires content, uninformative replies (abstention, chatter) are verifier-optimal for every context; therefore verifier-only selection, rejection-sampling fine-tuning and iterated self-training (expert iteration, ReST-EM style) move mass towards uninformative replies, and in the iterated limit the uninformative mass tends to one unless a coverage term is present. Give the odds-update per round in closed form. Then analyse best-of-n first-pass selection with the same base distribution: derive the selected distribution over informative/uninformative replies and show what happens to Q on answerable requests and to the abstention share. Reconcile with the screening observation that best-of-4 lowered Q for the 3B model, and state which experimental control (same sampling temperature; best-of-1 baseline) the analysis demands. Be honest if the simple model predicts something different from the data and say what that implies.
3. **The training objective.** Write filtered SFT as the M-step of expert iteration with a binary weight; act-gated filtering as a different reward F·U; act balancing as importance weighting with the estimator. Write preference optimisation (DPO) with pairs from the same prompt and a mixture parameter λ for losers that are faithful-but-evasive versus unfaithful; state what the implicit reward learns under each λ and the predicted effect on the (over-abstention, leakage) frontier. Formulate the trade-off as constrained optimisation (maximise usefulness subject to a leakage budget ε) and relate the filters to points on that frontier. Propose how to combine verifier-only and act-filtered data to address the screening finding that act-tuned models lost faithfulness on held-out stories.
4. **Estimation and inference.** The independent unit (root simulation seed); paired differences on identical probes; cluster bootstrap with the resampling scheme written out; exact McNemar for paired binary outcomes; hierarchical resampling over training seeds; pre-registered primary contrasts, non-inferiority margin for F and superiority target for Q, Holm correction; a power calculation or the procedure to estimate it from development discordance rates.
5. **Oracle error.** The verifier is an imperfect classifier. Give the misclassification-corrected estimator of the true unfaithfulness rate from the observed rate and the audited sensitivity and specificity (Rogan–Gladen or equivalent), with its variance, and say how the human audit feeds it. State what the per-label precision and recall must be for the primary contrast to survive correction.
6. **Minimal pairs as counterfactual tests.** Define pair success and a sensitivity measure (the reply changes when and only when the intervention changes what the speaker knows), and relate it to the single-probe metrics.
7. **Transfer.** Define held-out seeds, held-out story families and held-out wording families; write the transfer hypotheses as inequalities with margins; name the confounds (shared templates, teacher exposure).
8. **Literature with sources:** selective prediction and risk–coverage (Geifman and El-Yaniv), reject option and Chow's rule, ReST-EM and RAFT, DPO, misclassification correction, cluster bootstrap, minimal-pair evaluation, abstention benchmarks. Cite with URLs you opened.
