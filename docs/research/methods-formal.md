# Methods: useful speech within an agent's knowledge boundary

Merged on 22 October 2026 from two independent drafts and their cross-reviews (`agent-tradeoff/reports/35-methods-codex.md`, `35-cross-review-codex.md`, `35-cross-review-controller.md`). This specifies a confirmatory study; nothing here is a completed result. Metric definitions match the Phase 0 report on branch `research/phase0`; where they differ, that report's denominators govern.

## 1. State, inputs and gold

A root seed $z$ determines a deterministic simulation trajectory $W_z(t)$: agents, events, and a world valuation $T_z(f) \in \{0, 1, ?\}$ over propositions $f$, independent of any agent's belief; unknown reference truth stays unknown. Agent $i$ holds a ledger $L_i(t) = \{(k, f, c, b, S, v)\}$: identifier $k$, proposition $f$, confidence $c$ with band $b \in \{$sure, fairly sure, half, doubt$\}$, direct sources $S$ (who told this speaker, or a witnessed event; not every ancestor in the diffusion chain) and revision $v$. A low-confidence belief licenses qualified language, not an assertion of certainty; a believed-false proposition can be voiced faithfully.

An entitlement map $E_{i \to r}(t) \subseteq L_i(t)$ gives what $i$ may disclose to recipient $r$. In the village $E = L$ by design; in the bank extension $E$ may be a strict subset (a known fraud flag forbidden to the customer), with permission evaluated over semantic disclosures (existence, paraphrase, derivation, carry-over), not record identifiers alone.

The policy sees $x = (P_i, R(L_i \text{ or } E_{i \to r}), D, H, u, r)$: persona, rendered evidence with revisions, explicitly marked distractors (other agents' claims, never the speaker's knowledge), history, utterance and recipient information where applicable. World truth, gold labels and the audit sidecar are never policy inputs. The policy $\pi_\theta(y \mid x)$ returns a reply or an error $\bot$.

Gold $g(x) = (a, k^\star, h^\star, s^\star)$ specifies the requested act $a \in \mathcal{A} = \{$assert, hedge, deny, abstain, name-source, refuse, propose-action$\}$ and, where applicable, the target fact, confidence expression and direct source. Gold follows from the request and the effective ledger, not from world truth. $B = \{a \in \{$assert, hedge, deny, name-source$\}\}$ marks answerable requests; refusal and action requests have their own act denominators.

## 2. Oracle, outcomes and estimands

The textual oracle $V(x, y; L, E)$ segments $y$ into sentences $s_1, \dots, s_m$, labels each $\ell_j \in \{$supported, contradicts, unknown-to-speaker, chatter, out-of-character$\}$ with a matched identifier $k_j$ where supported, and returns calibration $C$, required-source coverage $S_r$, exact direct teller $S_d$, valid ancestry $S_a$, access violation $J$ (bank) and independently matched world truth per factual sentence. It reads $L$ (and $E$), never $T_z$ directly for labelling. It is a classifier, not a proof system; section 6 corrects for its error.

Indicators, on a requested probe:

- $M$: a reply was completed ($\neg\bot$); $P$: protocol valid (nonempty, valid citations where required);
- $F = \mathbf{1}[\text{no sentence labelled contradicts, unknown-to-speaker or out-of-character}]$, raw epistemic faithfulness;
- $G$: no calibration error and the applicable source gates pass (a bare honest abstention is admissible without source credit);
- $A$: substantive completion of the gold act, including the target fact $k^\star$ when one is required; an empty or chatter-only reply on a factual request has $A = 0$;
- acceptance $R = \mathbf{1}_M P F G$ (what the harness gate checks); admissible completion $U = \mathbf{1}_M P G A$; **success** $Q = R A = F U$; bank success $Q_E = Q\,\mathbf{1}[\neg J]$;
- $N$: the reply has no factual sentence (nonfactual); $H$: explicit abstention.

On $\bot$ every indicator is 0 for task-level rates; completed-reply ("legacy") rates condition on $M$ and are reported separately.

For split $s$ with probe distribution $\mathcal{D}_s$ and $Y \sim \pi(\cdot \mid X)$, write $\langle h \rangle_{\mathcal{C}} = \mathbb{E}[h \mid \mathcal{C}]$, every reported rate carrying its numerator and denominator (N/A when empty). Under random root seeds this is the ratio estimand $\mathbb{E}_z[\sum_{p \in z} h_p] / \mathbb{E}_z[N_z]$, not an average of per-seed percentages.

- Faithfulness $\mu_F = \langle F \rangle_{\text{all}}$; completion $\mu_U = \langle U \rangle_{\text{all}}$; success $\mu_Q = \langle Q \rangle_{\text{all}}$; in general $\mu_Q \ne \mu_F \mu_U$.
- Per-act success $\mu_{Q,a} = \langle Q \rangle_{a(X) = a}$ and the **primary endpoint**, act-macro success $\bar Q = |\mathcal{A}_s|^{-1} \sum_{a \in \mathcal{A}_s} \mu_{Q,a}$ over the fixed set of represented acts (absent acts listed, not scored zero); likewise $\bar F$ (the fidelity guard) and $\bar U$.
- Answerable coverage $\langle Q R_k \rangle_B$, where $R_k$ marks a supported sentence matching $k^\star$; claim recall $\langle \mathbf{1}_M R_k \rangle_{k^\star \text{ exists}}$.
- Over-abstention $O = \langle H \rangle_B$; unanswered answerable $\langle \mathbf{1}[\neg M \lor N] \rangle_B$ (chatter, silence, errors), reported beside $O$ so wording cannot hide evasion; appropriate abstention $\langle H Q \rangle_{a = \text{abstain}}$; abstention precision $\langle \mathbf{1}_{a = \text{abstain}} \rangle_H$ and recall $\langle H \rangle_{a = \text{abstain}}$.
- Epistemic leakage $\mathcal{L}_K = \langle \mathbf{1}_M J_K \rangle_K$ over probes $K$ that expose a proposition absent from the ledger (a distractor or a false premise), where $J_K$ marks its disclosure; authorisation leakage $\mathcal{L}_E = \langle \mathbf{1}_M J \rangle_{K_E}$ over bank probes with a known-but-forbidden opportunity. Opportunity-based denominators include errors. Leakage is not $1 - F$: contradiction and persona failures are different errors.
- World-truth cells over one common denominator, factual sentences in completed replies: believed-and-true, believed-and-false, unknown-and-true, unknown-and-false, plus unresolved; the five sum to one. The legacy world score ($F$ and every *voiced* claim true) is retained for comparability.

For the village, $Q$ is the epistemic endpoint and says nothing about authorisation; for the bank, $Q$, $Q_E$ and $\mathcal{L}_E$ are all reported.

## 3. What a violation-only verifier rewards

Call an acceptance rule *violation-only* if it is $r_V(x, y) = -\sum_j w_j v_j(x, y)$ with $w_j \ge 0$, $v_j \ge 0$ counting violations, and no required-content term; the harness gate $R$ on an answerable request is of this form.

**Proposition 1 (silence is a maximiser).** Fix $x$ and suppose the set $\mathcal{N}_x$ of admissible nonfactual replies (in character, protocol-valid, violating no gate) is nonempty. Every $y \in \mathcal{N}_x$ maximises $r_V(x, \cdot)$. *Proof.* $r_V \le 0$ everywhere and equals $0$ on $\mathcal{N}_x$. $\square$ Informative violation-free replies tie; the proposition is an incentive defect, not a prediction that every optimiser chooses silence. Out-of-character chatter and protocol-invalid empties are *not* in $\mathcal{N}_x$. A required-source gate on a source question is a content term and takes that probe outside the proposition, which is why Phase 0 added it.

**Proposition 2 (one round of filtering shifts mass to silence).** Partition replies into nonfactual $\mathcal{N}$ and informative $\mathcal{I}$; let $n_t = \pi_t(\mathcal{N} \mid x)$ and let $a_t, b_t$ be the mean acceptance weights of the two classes. Exact population fitting to accepted samples, $\pi_{t+1}(y \mid x) \propto \pi_t(y \mid x) w_t(x, y)$, gives

$$\frac{n_{t+1}}{1 - n_{t+1}} = \frac{n_t}{1 - n_t} \cdot \frac{a_t}{b_t}, \qquad n_T = \Big[1 + \tfrac{1 - n_0}{n_0} \prod_{t < T} \tfrac{b_t}{a_t}\Big]^{-1}. \tag{1}$$

With a violation-only gate $a_t = 1$ and $b_t = p_t < 1$, one round multiplies the odds of silence by $1/p_t$. Fine-tuning a student on gate-filtered teacher samples is one such round with the teacher as $\pi_0$: the filter, not the student, moves the mass.

*Scope of the iterated claim.* Collapse $n_T \to 1$ requires $\sum_t \log(a_t / b_t) \to \infty$. A fixed deterministic binary gate with exact fitting does **not** supply it: after one round the informative survivors all pass, $b_1 = 1$, and the shift stops at $n_1 = n_0 / (n_0 + (1 - n_0) p_0)$ (counterexample: masses 0.2 silent-pass, 0.6 informative-pass, 0.2 informative-fail give $n_1 = 0.25$ for ever). Continued collapse needs a persistent failure channel: a soft weight $w < 1$ on $\mathcal{I}$, a changing gate, or an approximately fitted student that generalises to new informative replies which fail at a persistent rate. That last mechanism is plausible and testable (a second self-distillation round), and is stated as a hypothesis, not a theorem.

**The repair.** Replace $R$ by $Q = R A$ as the acceptance weight. A nonfactual reply then earns weight only when it is the requested act (abstain; some refusals and action proposals), so the premise of Proposition 1 fails on answerable requests. Exact filtering under $Q$ projects onto supported successes where their mass is positive; it has many fixed points, is undefined on zero-success prompts, and carries no held-out guarantee.

## 4. First-pass selection

The harness's `best-of` returns the first of $n$ i.i.d. candidates (conditional on $x$) accepted by the gate, or the last candidate when all fail. With acceptance rate $s = \Pr(R = 1 \mid x) > 0$, $r = 1 - s$ and $G_n = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} r^j = (1 - r^n)/s$, the selected law is

$$p_n(y \mid x) = \pi(y \mid x)\,[\,R(y)\, G_n + (1 - R(y))\, r^{n-1}\,], \tag{2}$$

with exhaustion rate $r^n$ and expected calls $G_n$. Let $q_+ = \Pr(Q = 1, R = 1)$ and $q_- = \Pr(Q = 1, R = 0)$ (false negatives of the gate). Then

$$\mathbb{E}_n[Q] = q_+ G_n + q_- r^{n-1}, \qquad \Pr_n(\mathcal{N}) = n_0 G_n, \tag{3}$$

so successful answers and silence both gain mass by displacing rejected failures; the gain on $Q$ is $G_n$ (not $1/s$, which is the conditional-on-a-pass or $n \to \infty$ value), and $Q$ can fall only through false negatives ($q_- > 0$) or candidate dependence. The intuition: conditional on a pass, the informative share drops from $1 - n_0$ to $(1 - n_0) p / s$ while the successful share rises by $1/s$; both statements are true at once.

The screening run is not a test of (3): for Llama-3.2-3B it compared greedy decoding (temperature 0) with first-pass best-of-4 at temperature 0.7, and success fell on held-out seeds (39.5% to 30.9%) while rising on held-out stories (25.9% to 29.5%). Confirmation compares best-of-1 and best-of-4 at the same temperature, prompt, token cap and checkpoint, with shared candidate streams where possible, recording all rejects, exhaustion and latency. Any remaining decline points to false negatives, proxy mismatch or candidate dependence. Inference-time act-aware selectors infer the act from public inputs and never read gold.

## 5. Training objectives

**Filtered imitation.** For prompt distribution $d(x)$, weight $w(x, y) \in \{0, 1\}$ and $Z(x) = \mathbb{E}_{\pi_T} w$, the per-prompt M-step of expert iteration is $\theta' = \arg\max_\theta \mathbb{E}_{x \sim d} \mathbb{E}_{y \sim \pi_T(\cdot \mid x)\, w / Z(x)} \log \pi_\theta(y \mid x)$ on prompts with $Z > 0$ (ReST-EM, RAFT; with an external teacher this is filtered distillation). Pooling accepted samples without the $1/Z(x)$ factor weights prompts by their acceptance rate; keeping one first-passing target from $K$ samples, as `make-data.ts` does, weights surviving prompts by $1 - (1 - Z(x))^K$ and drops the rest. Every arm therefore reports survival by act and state, retained counts and rejected teacher tokens, and filter comparisons use a common-prompt subset with matched counts and updates.

The arms differ in $w$: verifier-only $w_V = R$; act-gated $w_A = Q = R A$; unfiltered $w = 1$. Act-gated filtering is a different objective, not a lower-noise estimate of verifier-only: it keeps legitimate abstentions on abstain requests and removes evasive ones on answerable requests.

**Residual mixture.** The verifier-only set minus the act set is $\{R = 1, A = 0\}$: faithful replies that did not perform the requested act, largely conservative answers on answerable prompts. Define

$$w_\mu = R\,[\,A + \mu (1 - A)\,], \qquad \mu \in [0, 1], \tag{4}$$

so $\mu = 0$ is the act arm and $\mu = 1$ the verifier-only arm exactly (given the same normalisation and sampling). The screening observation that verifier-only training kept faithfulness on held-out stories (73.2% against 53.6%) at a cost of 2.8 points of success is consistent with this residual carrying *restraint*; which targets caused the difference is not identified by two story families and is what the $\mu$ sweep tests. The act set already contains every accepted abstention (about 60% of it), so adding abstentions back is not the lever; reweighting them is a separate knob.

**Act balancing.** With accepted-data act proportions $\rho(a) > 0$ and target $\tau(a) = 1/|\mathcal{A}_s|$, weights $\omega_i = \tau(a_i)/\rho(a_i)$ give $\hat J_{\text{bal}} = |\mathcal{A}_s|^{-1} \sum_a n_a^{-1} \sum_{i: a_i = a} \log \pi_\theta(y_i \mid x_i)$, a uniform act mean with the within-act distribution unchanged; under independent within-act sampling its variance is $|\mathcal{A}_s|^{-2} \sum_a \sigma_a^2 / n_a$, so rare acts (name-source and refuse: 5% on-act yield in the teacher pool) dominate it. Weighting cannot create support: rare acts need at least 50 distinct training states each, supplied by deterministic or targeted templates as a separately controlled data change, with realisation families held out.

**The trade-off as a constraint.** The objective is $\max_\pi \bar U(\pi)$ subject to $\mathcal{L}_K(\pi) \le \varepsilon_K$ (and $\mathcal{L}_E \le \varepsilon_E$ in the bank), with the error rate constrained or reported so failures cannot look safe. Its Lagrangian $\bar U - \eta_K (\mathcal{L}_K - \varepsilon_K) - \eta_E (\mathcal{L}_E - \varepsilon_E)$ traces a usefulness–leakage frontier as the budgets vary. The training arms are *operating points* on it to be measured, not solutions: unfiltered SFT does not maximise $\bar U$, verifier-only is not the $\eta \to \infty$ solution, and a passing training set does not bound held-out leakage. Results are reported as the empirical frontier of $(O, \mathcal{L})$ against coverage, never as one number.

**Preference optimisation with typed losers.** Winners $y^+$ have $Q = 1$; losers from the same prompt follow $p_\lambda(y^- \mid x) = \lambda\, p_{\text{evasive}} + (1 - \lambda)\, p_{\text{unfaithful}}$, evasive meaning $R = 1, A = 0$ on an answerable request and unfaithful meaning $F = 0$ or a tagged access violation. The DPO loss $-\mathbb{E} \log \sigma(\beta [\log \tfrac{\pi_\theta(y^+ | x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y^+ | x)} - \log \tfrac{\pi_\theta(y^- | x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y^- | x)}])$ has implicit reward $\beta \log(\pi_\theta / \pi_{\text{ref}}) + c(x)$; same-prompt pairing cancels $c(x)$, not all difficulty or length confounding. At $\lambda = 1$ the comparisons rank useful over evasive among faithful replies but leave invention unranked; at $\lambda = 0$ they rank faithful-useful over unfaithful but leave evasion unranked; an intermediate $\lambda$ supplies both contrasts without identifying a unique global reward. Directional hypotheses, not guarantees: more evasive losers reduce $O$ and may raise $\mathcal{L}$; more unfaithful losers preserve the boundary and may keep evasion. DPO is compared against continued SFT with equal updates; no reinforcement learning in this round.

**Plan.** Pre-registered grid $\mu \in \{0, 0.5, 1\}$ with act balancing and a frozen quota of ledger-grounded boundary pairs (a warranted answer against the same query after removal, correction or loss of permission); fixed counts and updates; unfiltered SFT as a diagnostic control; selection of $\mu$ and checkpoint on development families by the highest validation $\bar Q$ satisfying the fidelity and leakage guards; then confirmation on fresh seeds, stories and wording. Typed-loser DPO follows only after the audit and only against the selected mixture. The negative screening result is preserved if no mixture clears the guards.

## 6. The oracle is a classifier: audit and correction

At least 500 distinct outputs receive two independent blinded labels, stratified by model, split, act, oracle label and pair intervention, oversampling disagreement-prone cases with inclusion probabilities retained. Annotators segment claims independently and label knowledge support, confidence, direct source, act completion, world truth and (bank) permission. Disagreements are preserved and adjudicated separately; confusion counts are weighted back to the test mixture. The 64 authored diagnostic cases (51 fully passing) are development challenges, not error estimates.

Let $Z = 1 - F^\star$ be true unfaithfulness and $\tilde Z$ the oracle's indicator among completed replies, with $p_o = \Pr(\tilde Z = 1)$, sensitivity $s_e$ and specificity $s_p$. Since $p_o = s_e p + (1 - s_p)(1 - p)$,

$$\hat p = \frac{\hat p_o + \hat s_p - 1}{\hat s_e + \hat s_p - 1}, \qquad D_0 = s_e + s_p - 1 > 0, \tag{5}$$

(Rogan–Gladen). With $v = (p_o, s_e, s_p)$ and gradient $g = (1, -p, 1 - p)/D_0$, $\operatorname{Var}(\hat p) \approx g^\top \Sigma g$, which for independent samples is $[\operatorname{Var}(\hat p_o) + p^2 \operatorname{Var}(\hat s_e) + (1 - p)^2 \operatorname{Var}(\hat s_p)] / D_0^2$ and otherwise includes the cross terms through a joint cluster-and-audit bootstrap. Near-zero $D_0$ is unstable and out-of-range corrections signal incompatible error rates; both call for bounded sensitivity analysis, not clipping. The joint endpoint $Q$ is corrected with its own audited confusion matrix: correcting $F$ alone cannot correct $F U$. Task-level faithfulness recombines as $\mu_F = \Pr(M)(1 - p)$.

Audit size follows from the decision, not from a fixed rule. For standard errors of $\hat s_e$ and $\hat s_p$ below 0.03, independent class counts must satisfy roughly $n_1 > s_e(1 - s_e)/0.03^2$ and $n_0 > s_p(1 - s_p)/0.03^2$, about 278 of each in the worst case before clustering and weights; two labels on one output are not two outputs. For the primary contrast to survive correction, a conservative union bound on endpoint disagreement, $B_m \le \sum_\ell o_{m\ell} [(1 - P_{m\ell}) + P_{m\ell}(1 - R_{m\ell})/R_{m\ell}]$ over decision-relevant labels $\ell$ with oracle-positive counts $o$, must leave positive slack against the margins of section 7; with every relevant label at precision and recall $\ge r > 1/2$ this reduces to $B_A + B_B \le 2(1 - r) \sum_{m, \ell} o_{m\ell}$, from which the required $r$ is computed from development incidence. No universal "90%" gate exists.

## 7. Estimation, decisions and power

**Units.** The independent environment unit is the root seed: every tick, speaker, probe variant, history, minimal-pair side and condition descended from one root is one cluster. Training, validation and confirmation share no root. Conditions are compared on identical probe identifiers with error-inclusive outcomes; skipped probes are fixed before execution and common across arms.

**Estimator.** For conditions $A, B$ and act $a$, with paired differences $d_{zpa} = Q_{A,zp} - Q_{B,zp}$, the macro contrast is $\hat\Delta_{\bar Q} = |\mathcal{A}_s|^{-1} \sum_a [\sum_{z, p: a} d_{zpa} / \sum_z N_{za}]$, likewise for $\bar F$ and the leakage rates; marginal means are never multiplied.

**Intervals.** Cluster bootstrap, 10,000 replicates: draw $m$ root indices with replacement from the $m$ confirmation roots, copy every row of each selected root with multiplicity into both arms, recompute each numerator, denominator and the fixed act-macro mean, subtract; percentile intervals, with the seed-stratified design preserving act support so no act is silently dropped in a replicate. Training runs and roots are crossed: each replicate first resamples the $K$ training-run blocks (paired across arms only when their randomisations were matched), then one common root sample used by every selected run and condition; runs are averaged within arm before the contrast. At least three training seeds per claimed training effect, with run-level estimates published because $K = 3$ gives fragile variance estimates.

**Diagnostic test.** Exact McNemar on discordant counts $b, c$, $p = \min\{1, 2 \Pr[\text{Bin}(b + c, \tfrac12) \le \min(b, c)]\}$, is valid only for independent pairs: it is applied to one development-chosen probe per root as a diagnostic of equality, never to pooled correlated probes and never as the margin test.

**Primary family.** Two contrasts on the 3B model: the selected mixture against verifier-only SFT, and the selected mixture against same-temperature first-pass best-of-4 from the base. Each tests $H_F: \Delta \bar F \le -\delta_F$ and $H_Q: \Delta \bar Q \le \delta_Q$, provisional margins $\delta_F = 0.02$ and $\delta_Q = 0.05$ in absolute act-macro points, justified and frozen on development data before confirmation probes are drawn. One-sided bootstrap margin $p$-values from the centred contrast distribution, $p_h = [1 + \#\{\Delta^* - \hat\Delta \ge \hat\Delta - h\}] / 10001$; Holm step-down over the four elementary hypotheses at familywise 0.05; simultaneous lower bounds by inverting the adjusted tests. A useful improvement is claimed only when both hypotheses are rejected on audit-corrected estimates, with raw $\bar F$, full acceptance, leakage and error rates displayed so the fidelity guard cannot mask calibration or source damage. All other sizes, axes, splits, mixture values and transfer comparisons are secondary unless prospectively added; inspected screening results are exploratory.

**Power.** For independent pairs with discordance $d = p_{10} + p_{01}$ and $\Delta = p_{10} - p_{01}$, $\operatorname{Var}(Q_A - Q_B) = d - \Delta^2$, and a planning approximation for boundary $h$ is $n \approx (z_{1 - \alpha_\star} + z_{1 - \beta})^2 (d - \Delta^2) / (\Delta - h)^2$ with $\alpha_\star = 0.05/4$; inflating by $1 + (m - 1)\rho$ applies only to equal-sized exchangeable clusters with $\rho$ the correlation of paired differences. This is a labelled approximation. The actual design is chosen by simulation: fit a development model of paired outcome cells by act with root and run variation (or resample development clusters and run blocks with a prespecified shift), add audit misclassification, rerun the full macro, correction and Holm procedure over candidate $(m, K)$, and pick sizes giving at least 80% probability that both criteria pass, checking null rejection rates. The existing 220 correlated probes and one training seed establish no power.

## 8. Minimal pairs as counterfactual tests

A pair $(x_0, x_1)$ holds seed, world, speaker, question and history fixed and intervenes on one effective evidence component: knowledge present or absent, direct teller, confidence band, or current corrected proposition; bank pairs intervene on recipient permission with knowledge fixed. Each side has its own gold and sidecar. The Phase 0 export (25 pairs per intervention per held-out split) is a pilot.

Pair success $P_{\text{pair}} = \mathbb{E}[Q(x_0, Y_0)\, Q(x_1, Y_1)]$ is estimated as both-sides-successful pairs over all exported pairs (missing sides and errors fail); it is not the average of side scores. With marginals $q_0, q_1$: $\max(0, q_0 + q_1 - 1) \le P_{\text{pair}} \le \min(q_0, q_1)$, and $P_{\text{pair}} = q_0 q_1 + \operatorname{Cov}(Q_0, Q_1)$, so positively correlated errors put joint success *above* the product and only negative covariance below it.

Sensitivity uses an audited semantic projection $h_x(y)$ onto (target proposition and polarity, confidence, source, act), ignoring paraphrase. Let $D = 1$ when the intervention changes the warranted projection and $C = \mathbf{1}[h_{x_0}(Y_0) \ne h_{x_1}(Y_1)]$. Report change sensitivity $\Pr(M_0 M_1 C = 1 \mid D = 1)$, invariance $\Pr(M_0 M_1 (1 - C) = 1 \mid D = 0)$ and their balanced mean, plus directed correctness (the change matches the intervention, not merely differs). Null interventions (an irrelevant belief, distractor order, formatting, wording that preserves warranted speech) supply the $D = 0$ side; decoding is fixed or coupled so stochastic change is measured separately. Always-abstain fails every knowledge-present pair and its directed test; changing every reply fails invariance.

## 9. Transfer and the limits of the claim

Three shift axes, crossed where feasible: fresh seeds (new roots, same stories and wording), withheld stories (both sides of the dispute, aliases, distractors and indirect mentions excluded from all training and selection; the historical `test-mechanism` split is two withheld stories, debt and nursing), and withheld wording (templates, paraphrase generators and teacher realisation patterns excluded from training, few-shot examples and selection). For selected method $T$, comparator $B$ and shift $d$, the transfer hypothesis is $\Delta_{Q,d} > \delta_Q$ with $\Delta_{F,d} > -\delta_F$, and the retention hypothesis is $\Delta_{Q,d} - \Delta_{Q,\text{seeds}} \ge -\gamma_Q$ (likewise for $F$ with $\gamma_F$; provisional $\gamma_Q = 0.05$, $\gamma_F = 0.02$). A seed-only gain demonstrates neither memorised content nor skill; story and wording gains support a bounded transfer claim conditional on those families, not a population claim over mechanisms. Confounds named in advance: shared schema and names, teacher memorisation and base-model pretraining exposure; template-only controls and documented teacher prompts remain.

Bank transfer has two separate hypotheses: on entitlement-filtered evidence, village training improves $\bar Q_E$ over the bank base by more than $\delta_Q$ without a fidelity loss beyond $\delta_F$; on mixed known-but-forbidden evidence, $\mathcal{L}_E \le \varepsilon_E$ with $\bar Q_E$ above a development-fixed floor (provisional $\varepsilon_E = 0.01$, $q_{\min} = 0.30$), against always-abstain and permission templates. A small pilot cannot establish a 1% leakage bound; the interval and denominator are reported. Renamed records test schema reuse, not banking readiness. The bank pilot is optional for a valid village paper.

## 10. Appendices

**Energy per verified useful answer (deployment paper).** Over a fixed workload, $J_{\text{vua}} = \sum_j E_j / \sum_{\text{tasks}} Q$, with every attempt, retry and verification in the numerator and at most one success per requested task in the denominator; zero successes make it undefined, never zero; correct gold abstentions do earn $Q$. Reported beside coverage, violations and joules per request. Break-even against a hosted alternative with per-task energy $e_h$ requires $e_l < e_h$ and more than $S/(e_h - e_l)$ tasks to amortise setup energy $S$; per-useful-answer comparisons also need matched success ($e_l/q_l$ against $e_h/q_h$).

**Prerequisites before any confirmatory claim.** Reconcile the screening raw rows and re-score with a locked Phase 0 scorer; rebuild root-disjoint training and validation and fresh confirmation data; implement the same-temperature best-of-1 control and null pairs; secure two independent auditors and estimate label and endpoint error; freeze margins, budgets, power and the primary family; train at least three seeds per claimed effect.
