# How it works

## What you do

Rumourtown is an agentic game. Walk to someone, whisper a rumor and follow the red thread. Read people’s tags to see what they believe and why. Read the ending when the day is done. Each villager is an autonomous agent with goals, a routine, memory, beliefs and decisions. Their actions are not scripted.

## How villagers think

Villagers remember beliefs and who told them. Trust and repetition change what they believe. Corrections help but leave a residue. Seeing beats hearing. People take sides and make up. Every social rule draws on published research; some numbers are tuned for this game. The README’s rule table explains the evidence and limits.

[Read the rule table and research](../README.md#how-the-villagers-think).

## What the thread shows

The thread shows who told whom, in order. Faces follow the actual tellings of the rumor you choose. Tap a face to find that person. Hearing a story does not mean believing it.

## Sharing

A shared link records the seed, starting time and whispers for replay with the same engine and content. Long runs keep only the most recent whispers that fit the link's size limit. Confirmed stories keep your exact words in dialogue, research exports and replay links. Review the preview and avoid private details. See [replay details and limits](architecture.md#share-links).

## Your typed whisper and the budget

Your story keeps its exact wording. A small AI call can identify the actor, affected person and social meaning, and suggest a clarifying question. The event does not need to fit a fixed category. You review the wording and interpretation before sending. Confirmed stories appear in dialogue, research exports and shared replay links; avoid private details. If AI is unavailable or the budget runs out, the story remains playable with neutral meaning. Ongoing gossip runs locally.
